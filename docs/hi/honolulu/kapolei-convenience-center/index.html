Kapolei Convenience Center

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Honolulu, HI. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City and County of Honolulu
Full address
91-184 Kaomi Loop, Kapolei, HI
Accepted materials
Passenger or light truck tires, with or without rims (limit 4 per month); household rubbish, furniture, bulky items, large metal appliances (limit 2 per month), green waste. NOT accepted: heavy truck or equipment tires
Hours / collection schedule
Daily 7am–6pm (closed Christmas and New Year's Day)
Fees
not stated by source
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Residents only (no businesses); sedans, SUVs, pickups or minivans only, no trailers; 2 loads per day. Rules: https://www.honolulu.gov/env/ref/opala2-waste-drop-off-rules-residents/. Phone (808) 260-2801
