Hazardous waste drop-off in Honolulu, HI

Household hazardous waste is accepted only at bi-monthly, appointment-only drop-off events (12-item limit). Register via (808) 768-3201 or hhw@honolulu.gov. Source: https://www.honolulu.gov/env/ref/hhw-2/

Kapaʻa Transfer Station

100 Kapaʻa Quarry Road, Kailua, HI

Accepts: Household trash, bulky items, large metal appliances, tires, lead-acid batteries, lithium/NiMH/NiCad batteries, propane and helium tanks, fire extinguishers
Hours: Mon–Fri 10am–6pm
Notes: Residential waste only; sedans, SUVs, pickups, minivans

Source: https://www.honolulu.gov/env/ref/waste-drop-off-locations/ · Last checked 2026-11-02
