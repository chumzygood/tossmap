Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Honolulu, HI

Household hazardous waste is accepted only at appointment-only drop-off events (12-item limit, 200 appointments per event). The last 2026 event (Sat Nov 7) is full and registration is closed; the city lists 2027 dates as TBD (checked 2026-11-04). Register via (808) 768-3201 or hhw@honolulu.gov. Source: https://www.honolulu.gov/env/ref/hhw-2/

Kapaʻa Transfer Station

100 Kapaʻa Quarry Road, Kailua, HI

Accepts: Household trash, bulky items, large metal appliances, tires, lead-acid batteries, lithium/NiMH/NiCad batteries, propane and helium tanks, fire extinguishers
Hours: Mon–Fri 10am–6pm
Notes: Residential waste only; sedans, SUVs, pickups, minivans
