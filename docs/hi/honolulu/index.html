Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Honolulu, HI

Household chemical HHW is appointment-only at city drop-off events (200-appointment cap). The last 2026 event (Sat Nov 7) is closed at capacity; 2027 dates TBD. Call (808) 768-3201 or email hhw@honolulu.gov. Source: https://www.honolulu.gov/env/ref/hhw-2/

Kapaʻa Transfer Station

100 Kapaʻa Quarry Road, Kailua, HI

Accepts: Household trash, bulky items, large appliances, tires, lead-acid batteries, standalone lithium/lithium-ion/NiMH/NiCad batteries, propane and helium tanks, butane, MAPP, fire extinguishers, green waste
Hours: Mon–Fri 10am–6pm; Sat–Sun 7am–6pm (closed Christmas and New Year's Day)
Notes: Phone (808) 768-4248. Residential drop-off rules apply.
