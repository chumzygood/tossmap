T&N Computer Recycling (South Beretania Street)

Honolulu residents can drop off electronics at T&N Computer Recycling (South Beretania Street), Mon–Fri 9:15am–4:45pm; Sat 8am–3pm; closed for lunch 12:30–1pm. Source: health.hawaii.gov.

T&N Computer Recycling (South Beretania Street) is at 1436 S Beretania Street, Honolulu, HI 96814. Hours: Mon–Fri 9:15am–4:45pm; Sat 8am–3pm; closed for lunch 12:30–1pm. It accepts covered electronic devices under Hawaii's Electronic Device Recycling and Recovery Law: computers, monitors, laptops, tablets and e-readers, printers, TVs (9 in. screen or larger), fax machines, VCRs, DVD players and recorders, portable digital music players, household routers and modems, and peripherals (keyboards, mice, cords, power supplies and adapters, computer or TV speakers and sound bars, video game consoles). This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
