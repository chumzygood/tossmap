Where can I drop off hazardous waste in Omaha (Under the Sink)?

Omaha's household hazardous waste drop-off is Under The Sink at 4001 S 120th St, Omaha, NE 68137. Hours recorded: Wed 9am–4:45pm; Thu 9am–6:15pm; Fri 9am–4:45pm; Sat by appointment. TossMap lists 1 official drop-off site in Omaha, NE. Listed materials include paint, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals. Under The Sink is the Omaha region's household chemicals disposal facility. Open to Douglas and Sarpy County households only; no waste from farms, businesses, offices, schools or institutions. Saturday drop-off by appointment only (home must be in Douglas or Sarpy County). Holiday schedule: closed Thu–Sat Nov 26–28, 2026 and Dec 25, 2026. 402-444-7465. Last checked 2026-11-24. Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.
