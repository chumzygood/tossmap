Under The Sink

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Omaha, NE. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
not stated by source
Full address
4001 S 120th St, Omaha, NE 68137
Accepted materials
Household hazardous waste: latex and oil-based paint, spray paint, paint thinner and remover, stains and varnish; pesticides, weed killers and garden chemicals; pool chemicals; cleaning products, drain and oven cleaners; motor oil, oil filters, antifreeze, gasoline and automotive fluids; automotive batteries, lithium and rechargeable batteries; fluorescent tubes and CFLs; mercury devices; propane bottles up to 1 lb; sharps in a rigid labeled container. Not accepted: business waste, single-use (alkaline/button) batteries, electronics, tires, appliances, medications, fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, propane tanks over 1 lb
Hours / collection schedule
Wed 9am–4:45pm; Thu 9am–6:15pm; Fri 9am–4:45pm; Sat by appointment
Fees
Free, except latex paint and water-based coatings: $1.00 per container, quart size and larger (credit card only)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Under The Sink is the Omaha region's household chemicals disposal facility. Open to Douglas and Sarpy County households only; no waste from farms, businesses, offices, schools or institutions. Saturday drop-off by appointment only (home must be in Douglas or Sarpy County). Holiday schedule: closed Thu–Sat Nov 26–28, 2026 and Dec 25, 2026. 402-444-7465
