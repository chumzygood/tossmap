Haz Bin

Haz Bin in Louisville, KY accepts paint, batteries and household chemicals, Tue–Sat 9:30am–4pm, and it's free for Louisville/Jefferson County residents. Source: louisvilleky.gov.

Haz Bin is at 7501 Grade Ln, Louisville, KY 40219. Hours: Tue–Sat 9:30am–4pm. It accepts household hazardous materials including asbestos floor tiles (less than 25 lbs), automotive fluids, batteries (auto, household and rechargeable), cleaners, driveway sealers, fertilizers, fire extinguishers, fluorescent and LED bulbs, Freon, furniture polishes and waxes, gasoline (including boat gas), hearing-aid and watch batteries, kerosene, lawn and garden hazardous chemicals (container must be left at the Haz Bin), mercury thermometers and thermostats, metal polishes and rust removers, mildew removers, mineral spirits/turpentine, mothballs, nail polish and removers, oil-based or lead paint, oven and drain cleaners, pesticides, herbicides, fungicides and insecticides (container must be left at the Haz Bin), propane tanks (grill and camping size), smoke detectors, solvents and degreasers, spray paint, swimming pool chemicals, vapes and e-cigarettes, varnishes and stains, and wood preservatives. Not accepted: items from businesses; containers larger than 5 gallons or 40 pounds; more than 25 lbs of asbestos; helium tanks; ammunition; expired medication; or latex paint. Fees: Free for Louisville/Jefferson County residents. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
