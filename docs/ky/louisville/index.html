Haz Bin

7501 Grade Ln, Louisville, KY 40219

Accepts: Household hazardous waste (paint, chemicals, pesticides, automotive fluids, batteries, bulbs)
Hours: Tue–Sat 9:30am–4pm

Waste Reduction Center

636 Meriwether Avenue, Louisville, KY 40217 (entrance on Bland Street)

Accepts: Passenger car tires (up to 4/day free for residents); electronics (up to 3 items/day free for residents; computers, monitors, TVs, printers, phones, game systems); up to 4 large household items/day; truck tires (fee)
Hours: Tue–Fri 8am–12pm & 1–4:30pm; Sat 8am–3pm
Fees: Residents: 4 tires, 3 electronics and 4 large items per day free; business passenger tires $4 each; truck tires $10 ($25 on rims); extra electronics $0.60/lb by appointment; debit/credit only
Notes: Louisville/Jefferson County residents and businesses; holidays may change hours (closed Thanksgiving Day and day after). 502-574-2796
