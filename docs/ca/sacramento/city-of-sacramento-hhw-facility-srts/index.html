City of Sacramento HHW Facility (Sacramento Recycling and Transfer Station)

Sacramento residents can drop off paint, electronics, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals at City of Sacramento HHW Facility (Sacramento Recycling and Transfer Station), Tue–Sat 8am–5pm. Source: cityofsacramento.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Sacramento, CA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Sacramento Recycling & Solid Waste Division
Full address
8491 Fruitridge Road, Sacramento, CA 95826
Accepted materials
Household hazardous waste: latex and oil-based paint, paint thinners, solvents; used motor oil and filters, antifreeze, brake/transmission fluid, automotive products, gasoline and other flammables; car batteries and household batteries; fluorescent lamps and tubes, mercury thermometers; pesticides and herbicides; pool chemicals; household cleaners; acids and bases; aerosol cans; kitchen oils; propane cylinders; flares; vape pens and e-cigarettes; sharps (special packaging); electronic waste (anything with a plug or that runs on batteries). Not accepted: air bags, ammunition, asbestos, compressed gas cylinders (except propane and handheld aerosols), explosives and fireworks, infectious and medical waste (except sharps), medications, radioactive and reactive waste, treated wood
Hours / collection schedule
Tue–Sat 8am–5pm
Fees
Free (maximum 15 gallons or 125 lbs per trip)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
City of Sacramento HHW drop-off at the Sacramento Recycling and Transfer Station (City of Sacramento Recycling & Solid Waste Division 2026–2027 Service Guide). Contractor or business waste: call to make an appointment. (916) 737-4880
