Elk Grove Special Waste Collection Center

Elk Grove Special Waste Collection Center in Elk Grove, CA accepts paint, electronics, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals. See hours below. Source: elkgrove.gov.

Elk Grove Special Waste Collection Center is at 9255 Disposal Lane, Elk Grove, CA 95624. Hours: Residents: Sat–Wed 9am–4pm. Reuse Room: Sat–Wed 9am–3:30pm (Elk Grove residents only). Small businesses: Wednesdays by appointment only. Closed all City holidays, including Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day. It accepts household hazardous waste: latex and oil-based paint, paint thinner and stripper, varnish; motor oil, antifreeze, transmission fluid, gasoline and diesel; auto and motorcycle batteries and household batteries; fluorescent and LED bulbs, mercury devices; pesticides, herbicides and fertilizer; pool chemicals; household cleaners; aerosol cans; cooking oil and grease; propane cylinders (5 gal or less); household fire extinguishers; smoke alarms; over-the-counter medicines; sharps in rigid containers; non-friable asbestos (special packaging, max 125 lb). Electronics: TVs (CRT, LCD, plasma), computers, laptops, monitors, tablets, cell phones, telephones, printers, copiers, fax machines, radios and stereos, VCR/DVD players. Not accepted: trash, mattresses, Styrofoam (EPS), explosives and ammunition, infectious medical waste, large compressed gas cylinders, radioactive waste, treated wood, prescription (controlled) medications. Fees: No fee for Sacramento County residents; proof of residency required (photo ID, utility bill or similar). This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
