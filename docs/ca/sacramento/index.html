Where can I drop off paint in Sacramento?

North Area Recovery Station HHW Drop-Off at 4450 Roseville Rd, North Highlands, CA is listed as accepting paint. Kiefer Landfill (tires, e-waste & ABOP drop-off) at 12701 Kiefer Blvd., Sloughhouse, CA 95683 is listed as accepting paint. The listing records acceptance as: Free residential drop-off: passenger/light-truck tires (rims OK, 4 per day free), e-waste (TVs, CRTs, monitors, computers, laptops, tablets, phones, printers, gaming consoles, stereos), large appliances (2 per day free), small corded appliances and power tools, mattresses and box springs, clean metal, clean inerts. ABOP & Special Waste facility (residents): antifreeze, household and car batteries, motor oil and filters, cooking oil, latex and oil-based paint, aerosol cans, brake/transmission fluid, fluorescent tubes and bulbs (12 tubes per month), home-generated sharps in sealed containers, mercury thermometers and switches, barbecue propane tanks (limit two). Not accepted at ABOP: acids, household cleaners, pesticides and herbicides, solvents, paint thinners, gasoline, pool chemicals, ammunition, explosives, medical waste. Products with built-in batteries go to the e-waste area. Also takes household trash, green waste and treated wood (fees). Operated by Sacramento County Department of Waste Management & Recycling. The landfill takes waste from the public, businesses and haulers, and customers do not need to be Sacramento County residents; free e-waste and ABOP drop-off are for residents. Businesses: ABOP and e-waste via the county's Commercial Hazardous Waste program by appointment (916-876-9458), for a fee. Fees: https://wmr.saccounty.gov/content/wmr/us/en/county-facilities/kiefer-landfill/fees.html; ABOP: https://wmr.saccounty.gov/content/wmr/us/en/county-facilities/kiefer-landfill/special-waste.html; free items: https://wmr.saccounty.gov/content/wmr/us/en/residential-services/free-drop-off-locations/free-drop-off-items.html. Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.
