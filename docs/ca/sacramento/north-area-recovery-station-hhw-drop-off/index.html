North Area Recovery Station HHW Drop-Off

Sacramento residents can drop off paint, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals at North Area Recovery Station HHW Drop-Off, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat 8:30am–4pm. Source: https://wmr.saccounty.gov/content/wmr/us/en/county-facilities/north-area-recovery-station/nars-hhw-drop-off.html.
