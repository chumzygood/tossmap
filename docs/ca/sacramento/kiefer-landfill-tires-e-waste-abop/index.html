Kiefer Landfill (tires, e-waste & ABOP drop-off)

Kiefer Landfill (tires, e-waste & ABOP drop-off) in Sloughhouse, CA accepts paint, electronics, tires, batteries and motor oil. See hours below. Source: wmr.saccounty.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Sacramento, CA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Sacramento County Department of Waste Management & Recycling
Full address
12701 Kiefer Blvd., Sloughhouse, CA 95683
Accepted materials
Free residential drop-off: passenger/light-truck tires (rims OK, 4 per day free), e-waste (TVs, CRTs, monitors, computers, laptops, tablets, phones, printers, gaming consoles, stereos), large appliances (2 per day free), small corded appliances and power tools, mattresses and box springs, clean metal, clean inerts. ABOP & Special Waste facility (residents): antifreeze, household and car batteries, motor oil and filters, cooking oil, latex and oil-based paint, aerosol cans, brake/transmission fluid, fluorescent tubes and bulbs (12 tubes per month), home-generated sharps in sealed containers, mercury thermometers and switches, barbecue propane tanks (limit two). Not accepted at ABOP: acids, household cleaners, pesticides and herbicides, solvents, paint thinners, gasoline, pool chemicals, ammunition, explosives, medical waste. Products with built-in batteries go to the e-waste area. Also takes household trash, green waste and treated wood (fees)
Hours / collection schedule
Landfill: Mon–Fri 6:30am–4:30pm; Sat–Sun 8:30am–4:30pm (arrive at least 15 minutes before closing; may close early if the daily vehicle limit is reached). Thanksgiving Day closes at 2pm; Christmas and New Year's Day close at 2pm on weekdays, all day on weekends. ABOP & Special Waste drop-off: Tue–Sat 8:30am–4pm (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day)
Fees
Free for residents: tires 1–4 per day, e-waste, appliances 1–2 per day, mattresses, clean metal, and ABOP items up to 15 gallons or 125 pounds per month (a load must contain only free items). Tires 5–8 per day $10 each; commercial/semi tires $30 each; trash loads $10 (cars) to $45 (large vans) per vehicle, or $61.35 per ton weighed (2026 fees)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Operated by Sacramento County Department of Waste Management & Recycling. The landfill takes waste from the public, businesses and haulers, and customers do not need to be Sacramento County residents; free e-waste and ABOP drop-off are for residents. Businesses: ABOP and e-waste via the county's Commercial Hazardous Waste program by appointment (916-876-9458), for a fee. Fees: https://wmr.saccounty.gov/content/wmr/us/en/county-facilities/kiefer-landfill/fees.html; ABOP: https://wmr.saccounty.gov/content/wmr/us/en/county-facilities/kiefer-landfill/special-waste.html; free items: https://wmr.saccounty.gov/content/wmr/us/en/residential-services/free-drop-off-locations/free-drop-off-items.html. (916) 875-5555
