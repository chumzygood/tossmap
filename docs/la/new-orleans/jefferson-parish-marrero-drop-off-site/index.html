Jefferson Parish Marrero Drop-Off Site

Waste and recycling drop-off information for New Orleans, LA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
not stated by source
Full address
6440 Lapalco Boulevard, Marrero, LA 70072
Accepted materials
Waste tires (5 per resident per day); motor oil (5 gal/day), oil filters (2/day), antifreeze (5 gal/day), gasoline (2 gal/day); lead-acid batteries; cooking oil (5 gal/day); e-waste: flat-screen TVs/monitors, computers, laptops, tablets, phones, printers, cable boxes, game consoles, networking gear, UPS units. Not accepted: CRT TVs/monitors, light bulbs, copiers, stereos, toner
Hours / collection schedule
Tue–Sun 9am–5:30pm (closed observed holidays)
Fees
Free for residents
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Free self-service site for Jefferson Parish residents (driver's license or water bill required); commercial users may not drop off tires, auto fluids, e-waste or batteries. Unload items yourself. (504) 731-4612
