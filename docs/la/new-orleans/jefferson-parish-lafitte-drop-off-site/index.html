Jefferson Parish Lafitte Drop-Off Site

Jefferson Parish Lafitte Drop-Off Site is a listed drop-off site in Jean Lafitte, LA, Tue–Sun 9am–5:30pm (closed observed holidays). Source: jeffparish.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for New Orleans, LA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Jefferson Parish Department of Environmental Affairs
Full address
Treasure Street, Jean Lafitte, LA 70067
Accepted materials
Garbage and trash; white goods; bulky waste; scrap metal; tree and yard waste; construction debris. No additional materials (no tires, auto fluids, e-waste or batteries at this site). Not accepted: CRT TVs/monitors, light bulbs, copiers, stereos, toner
Hours / collection schedule
Tue–Sun 9am–5:30pm (closed observed holidays)
Fees
Free for residents with curbside garbage collection in unincorporated Jefferson Parish and Jean Lafitte; contractors, commercial users and other Jefferson Parish municipalities (Kenner, Gretna, Westwego, Harahan, Grand Isle) pay $14 per pickup truck or $20 per trailer (card only, no cash)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Self-service: unload debris into roll-off containers yourself. Bring a driver's license or water bill to verify residence; residents are limited to 2 visits per day. Dump trailers and rented box trucks are not accepted, and Lafitte may limit trailer size further because the site is small. (504) 731-4612
