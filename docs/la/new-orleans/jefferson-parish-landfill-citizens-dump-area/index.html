Jefferson Parish Landfill (Citizens' Dump Area)

Jefferson Parish Landfill (Citizens' Dump Area) is a listed drop-off site in Waggaman, LA, Mon–Sat 6am–5pm; Sun 6am–noon. Source: jeffparish.gov.

Jefferson Parish Landfill (Citizens' Dump Area) is at 5800 Highway 90 West, Waggaman, LA 70094. Hours: Mon–Sat 6am–5pm; Sun 6am–noon. It accepts household garbage and trash from Jefferson Parish residences and small businesses (residents use the containers at the Citizens' Dump Area ramp). Not accepted: discarded appliances, tires, used automotive fluids, used cooking oil, lead-acid batteries, e-waste and residential recycling (Jefferson Parish sends these to its Metairie or Marrero drop-off sites). Fees: Residents: two free loads per day. Commercial waste (including small businesses, churches, schools and non-primary-residence property) pays a commercial disposal fee, $5 minimum, credit card or check only (no cash). This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
