Gretna Burmaster Waste Drop-Off Site

Waste and recycling drop-off information for New Orleans, LA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Gretna
Full address
1201 Burmaster Street, Gretna, LA 70053
Accepted materials
Bulk items: tree debris, furniture, appliances, miscellaneous trash, motor oil. NOT accepted: hazardous waste, tires, batteries, fuel, non-motor oil, electronics
Hours / collection schedule
Tue–Sat 7:30am–4pm; closed Sun–Mon
Fees
Free to Gretna residents; $35 gate fee for contractors and residents with small trailers (6 ft or less)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Operated by the City of Gretna. Replaced Jefferson Parish's Meadowbrook Drop-Off Site (484 Wall Blvd) on Nov 3, 2026; through a parish partnership all Jefferson Parish residents may use it free for residential waste (https://www.jeffparish.gov/1337/Drop-Off-Sites). City contact 504-363-1500
