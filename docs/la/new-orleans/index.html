Hazardous waste drop-off in New Orleans, LA

Ad slot (city-top)

Recycling Drop-Off Center

2829 Elysian Fields Ave, New Orleans, LA

Accepts: E-waste (computers, phones, monitors, printers, TVs limit 4), household batteries, light bulbs, tires (limit 5), glass (50 lbs), paper, plastics #1–2, cans
Hours: Saturdays 8am–1pm
Notes: Orleans Parish residents only. Household hazardous waste only on the annual HHW Collection Day (2026 date was April 11)

Source: https://nola.gov/recycling-drop-off/ · Last checked 2026-11-02
