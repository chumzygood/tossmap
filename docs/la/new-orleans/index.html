Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in New Orleans, LA

Recycling Drop-Off Center

2829 Elysian Fields Ave, New Orleans, LA

Accepts: E-waste (computers, laptops, phones, keyboards, monitors, printers, TVs limit 4, game consoles, cables, cameras); batteries (AA–D, 6V, 9V, lithium); light bulbs; tires (limit 5); glass (50 lbs); plastics #1–2; paper; cardboard; cans
Hours: Saturdays 8am–1pm
Fees: Free
Notes: Orleans Parish residents only; businesses excluded. Household chemical HHW is annual Collection Day only (2026 was April 11).

Jefferson Parish Metairie Drop-Off Site

400 David Drive, Metairie, LA 70003

Accepts: Waste tires (5 per resident per day); motor oil (5 gal/day), oil filters (2/day), antifreeze (5 gal/day), gasoline (2 gal/day); lead-acid batteries; cooking oil (5 gal/day); e-waste: flat-screen TVs/monitors, computers, laptops, tablets, phones, printers, cable boxes, game consoles, networking gear, UPS units. Not accepted: CRT TVs/monitors, light bulbs, copiers, stereos, toner
Hours: Tue–Sun 9am–5:30pm (closed observed holidays)
Fees: Free for residents
Notes: Free self-service site for Jefferson Parish residents (driver's license or water bill required); commercial users may not drop off tires, auto fluids, e-waste or batteries. Unload items yourself. (504) 731-4612
