Westpark Consumer Recycling Center

Houston residents can drop off paint, electronics, tires and batteries at Westpark Consumer Recycling Center, Tue–Sat 8am–5pm. Source: houstontx.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Houston, TX. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Houston Solid Waste Management Department
Full address
5900 Westpark, Houston, TX 77057
Accepted materials
Residential electronics (computers, laptops, monitors, printers, TVs including CRT TVs up to 36 in., cell phones, routers, cables, UPS units, copiers, fax machines, toner and ink cartridges); tires (up to 5, without rims); B.O.P.A. — batteries, oil, latex paint and antifreeze; scrap metal; recyclables (paper, cardboard, cartons, cans, glass, plastics #1–5 and #7). Not accepted: household hazardous waste chemicals, oil-based paint, heavy trash, garbage, alkaline household batteries in the electronics program
Hours / collection schedule
Tue–Sat 8am–5pm
Fees
Residential electronics accepted at no additional charge (other fees not stated)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
City of Houston Solid Waste Management Department. Proof of address: a current Texas-issued ID (driver's license, DPS ID, License to Carry or Consulate ID) or a current City of Houston utility bill or lease; for recycling only a current Texas-issued ID is sufficient. Residents may use the recycling centers up to 4 times per month (once per week); arrive at least 15–30 minutes before closing. The Recycling Centers page lists B.O.P.A. (batteries, oil, latex paint and antifreeze) as accepted but also lists “Hazardous materials: Chemicals, oil, fuel” as not accepted, so call 311 before bringing motor oil. Electronics details: https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/ewaste.
