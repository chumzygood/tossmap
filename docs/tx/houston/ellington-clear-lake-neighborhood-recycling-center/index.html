Ellington/Clear Lake Neighborhood Recycling Center

Houston residents can drop off electronics at Ellington/Clear Lake Neighborhood Recycling Center. See hours below. Source: houstontx.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Houston, TX. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Houston Solid Waste Management Department
Full address
246 Loop Rd., Houston, TX 77034
Accepted materials
Recyclables (paper, cardboard, cartons, cans, glass, plastics #1–5 and #7); residential electronics at the monthly 2nd-Saturday event (computers, laptops, monitors, printers, TVs including CRT TVs up to 36 in., cell phones, routers, cables, UPS units, copiers, fax machines, toner and ink cartridges). Not accepted at the electronics event: vacuum cleaners, CRT TVs over 36 in., radios, game consoles, microwaves and other appliances, fluorescent bulbs, smoke detectors, alkaline batteries, paint cans
Hours / collection schedule
Open 7 days a week 8am–8pm; electronics recycling event 2nd Saturday of each month 9am–3pm
Fees
not stated by source
Eligibility, ID and other notes
City of Houston Solid Waste Management Department. Proof of address: a current Texas-issued ID (driver's license, DPS ID, License to Carry or Consulate ID) or a current City of Houston utility bill or lease; for recycling only a current Texas-issued ID is sufficient. Residents may use the recycling centers up to 4 times per month (once per week); arrive at least 15–30 minutes before closing. Electronics are taken only at the monthly 2nd-Saturday event (details: https://www.houstontx.gov/solidwaste/ewaste). The Recycling Centers page also lists tires and B.O.P.A. in a general accepted-materials list without naming sites, so call 311 before bringing tires, oil or batteries.
