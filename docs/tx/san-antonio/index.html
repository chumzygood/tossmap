Bulky Waste Collection Center – Bitters

1800 Wurzbach Parkway, San Antonio, TX 78216

Accepts: Car tires (6 or fewer, no rims), large appliances, furniture, mattresses, carpeting, auto parts, water heaters & AC units, construction material (1 cu yd or less)
Hours: Tue–Fri 8am–5pm; Sat 8am–4pm
Fees: Free with City environmental fee
Notes: Residential customers only; bring a recent CPS Energy bill + photo ID; no household trash or hazardous material
