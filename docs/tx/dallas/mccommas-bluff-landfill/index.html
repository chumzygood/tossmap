McCommas Bluff Landfill

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Dallas, TX. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Dallas Sanitation Services
Full address
5100 Youngblood Road, Dallas, TX
Accepted materials
Tires (limit 6 per year from Dallas residents, not exceeding 25 inches in diameter); electronics (residents only); household waste, furniture, appliances (refrigerants removed), grass clippings/brush
Hours / collection schedule
Mon–Fri 5am–8pm; Sat 6am–4pm; closed Sun
Fees
Free for Dallas residents in passenger cars, station wagons, pickups and trailers under 15 ft hauling their own residential waste; commercial customers pay posted rates; uncovered loads $40
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Open to commercial and residential customers. Residents: current, valid Texas driver's license showing a Dallas address or a current Dallas water utilities bill. (214) 670-0977
