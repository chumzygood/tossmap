Northwest (Bachman) Transfer Station

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Dallas, TX. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Dallas Sanitation Services
Full address
9500 Harry Hines Boulevard, Dallas, TX
Accepted materials
Tires (limit 6 per year from Dallas residents, not exceeding 25 inches in diameter); electronics (residents only); recyclables, scrap metal, household waste, grass clippings. NOT accepted: chemicals, paint and other hazardous waste; refrigerators, freezers, AC units
Hours / collection schedule
Mon–Sat 7am–4:30pm; closed Sun
Fees
Free for Dallas residents in passenger cars, station wagons, pickups and trailers under 15 ft hauling their own residential waste
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Open only to Dallas residents and small commercial customers; valid driver's license plus proof of Dallas residency (Dallas address on the license or a current Dallas water bill). (214) 670-6161
