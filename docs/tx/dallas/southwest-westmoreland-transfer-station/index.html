Southwest (Westmoreland) Transfer Station

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Dallas, TX. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Dallas Sanitation Services
Full address
4610 S. Westmoreland Road, Dallas, TX
Accepted materials
Tires (limit 6 per year from Dallas residents, not exceeding 25 inches in diameter); electronics (residents only); recyclables, scrap metal, household waste, furniture, grass clippings/brush
Hours / collection schedule
Temporarily closed Oct 14–Nov 15, 2026 (reopens Mon Nov 16); then Mon–Fri 7am–9am; Sat 7am–4pm; closed Sun
Fees
Free for Dallas residents in passenger cars, station wagons, pickups and trailers under 15 ft hauling their own residential waste
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Dallas residents only; no commercial customers; valid driver's license plus proof of Dallas residency (Dallas address on the license or a current Dallas water bill). During the closure use another transfer station or McCommas Bluff Landfill. (214) 670-1927
