Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Fort Worth, TX

Environmental Collection Center

6400 Bridge St, Fort Worth, TX 76112

Accepts: Automotive fluids, batteries, cleaners/chemicals, paint, lawn & pool chemicals, bulbs, cooking oil
Hours: Thu–Fri 11am–7pm; Sat 9am–3pm

Brennan Drop-Off Station

2400 Brennan Ave, Fort Worth, TX

Accepts: Passenger tires (4 per household every 6 months, rims separated), computers & TVs (2 every 6 months), household chemicals incl. paint and motor oil (20 gal every 3 months), all batteries, light bulbs, appliances, bulky trash, recycling
Hours: Tue–Fri 8am–5pm; Sat 8am–noon; closed Sun–Mon and city holidays (incl. Thanksgiving and the day after)
Fees: Free for residents
Notes: Fort Worth residents with a City solid waste account: bring a water bill (within 6 months) + matching driver's license; apartment residents may bring recycling and HHW with a driver's license
