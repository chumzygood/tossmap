Where can I drop off paint in Fort Worth?

Environmental Collection Center at 6400 Bridge St, Fort Worth, TX 76112 is listed as accepting paint. Brennan Drop-Off Station, Southeast Drop-Off Station, Old Hemphill Road Drop-Off Station, and Hillshire (North Service Center) Drop-Off Station are listed as accepting paint. The listing records acceptance as: Passenger tires (4 per household every 6 months, rims separated), computers & TVs (2 every 6 months), household chemicals incl. paint and motor oil (20 gal every 3 months), all batteries, light bulbs, appliances, bulky trash, recycling. Fort Worth residents with a City solid waste account: bring a water bill (within 6 months) + matching driver's license; apartment residents may bring recycling and HHW with a driver's license. Last checked 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-05. Confirm current acceptance, hours and eligibility with the official source before you go. Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.
