Brennan Drop-Off Station

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Fort Worth, TX. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
not stated by source
Full address
2400 Brennan Ave, Fort Worth, TX
Accepted materials
Passenger tires (4 per household every 6 months, rims separated), computers & TVs (2 every 6 months), household chemicals incl. paint and motor oil (20 gal every 3 months), all batteries, light bulbs, appliances, bulky trash, recycling
Hours / collection schedule
Tue–Fri 8am–5pm; Sat 8am–noon; closed Sun–Mon and city holidays (incl. Thanksgiving and the day after)
Fees
Free for residents
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Fort Worth residents with a City solid waste account: bring a water bill (within 6 months) + matching driver's license; apartment residents may bring recycling and HHW with a driver's license
