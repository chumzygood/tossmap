Hanover Courthouse Convenience Center

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Richmond, VA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Hanover County
Full address
7234 Courtland Farm Road, Hanover, VA 23069
Accepted materials
Passenger vehicle tires up to 32 in. diameter (4 per day, no fee); used oil, oil filters, antifreeze, gasoline, automobile fluids and solvents; lead-acid batteries; household trash, bulky waste (furniture, appliances), recyclables, clothing. Liquid paint is not accepted for recycling (dry it to a paste and place it in the trash); electronics go to the county's e-waste collection events (typically spring and fall); grass and unbagged leaves; mattresses and box springs (limit 2 per day)
Hours / collection schedule
Daily 7am–7pm (closed listed county holidays)
Fees
No cost for residents' household trash and recyclables; up to 4 passenger tires per day without fee
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Hanover County residents only; bring ID or vehicle registration (staff may verify residency). No commercial vehicles, U-Hauls or box trucks; businesses must use the Route 301 Transfer Station. Closed New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Easter Sunday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Call Before You Haul: 804-365-3388. Tires/batteries/paint details: https://www.hanovercounty.gov/230/What-Can-Be-Recycled
