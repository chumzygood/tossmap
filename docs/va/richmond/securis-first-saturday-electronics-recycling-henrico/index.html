Securis First Saturday Electronics Recycling (CVWMA)

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Richmond, VA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Securis (Central Virginia Waste Management Authority electronics recycling contractor)
Full address
8419 Glazebrook Ave, Henrico, VA
Accepted materials
Electronics: computers, laptops, monitors and TVs (including CRTs), printers, cell phones and tablets, cables, game systems, stereos, networking gear, small household electronics, holiday lights. NOT accepted: alkaline batteries, light bulbs, mercury devices, items containing Freon, any liquids, chemicals, oils or powders
Hours / collection schedule
First Saturday of each month, 10am–2pm (next: Sat Dec 5, 2026)
Fees
Fees apply to some items (e.g. computers/laptops with hard drives $5, LCD TV $25, CRT TV $28–$35, LCD monitor $10); many items free. Credit or debit cards only (no cash or checks)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Central Virginia Waste Management Authority (CVWMA) contractor Securis runs this monthly event; CVWMA says it is open to all residents of Central Virginia. Item list and fee table: https://securis.com/events/richmond-recycling-events/
