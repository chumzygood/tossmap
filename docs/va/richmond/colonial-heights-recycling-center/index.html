Colonial Heights Recycling Center

Richmond residents can drop off paint, batteries and motor oil at Colonial Heights Recycling Center, Fri, Sat & Mon 8am–5pm (closed for lunch 12–1pm). Source: colonialheightsva.gov.

Colonial Heights Recycling Center is at 2701 Conduit Road, Colonial Heights, VA 23834. Hours: Fri, Sat & Mon 8am–5pm (closed for lunch 12–1pm). It accepts category 1 household hazardous waste: latex and oil-based paints, varnish/shellac, stains and tints, lacquer thinners, polyurethane, primers, paint reducers, Varsol, mineral spirits, turpentine, other flammable solvents, leaded and unleaded gasoline and additives, cooking oil, motor oil, oil additives, oil filters, antifreeze. Also batteries, brush, tree limbs, leaves and grass, appliances, air conditioners, metals, cardboard. Not accepted: insecticides, poisons, acids and other caustic compounds, PCB-containing appliances; electronic devices (TVs, cell phones, computers, monitors) are prohibited. Fees: $10 Freon (CFC) removal fee per appliance, paid in advance at the Billing and Collections Office (201 James Avenue); other fees not stated by source. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
