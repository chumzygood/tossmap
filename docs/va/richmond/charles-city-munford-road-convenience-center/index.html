Charles City County Munford Road Convenience Center

Charles City County Munford Road Convenience Center in Providence Forge, VA accepts paint, batteries and motor oil. See hours below. Source: charlescityva.us.

Charles City County Munford Road Convenience Center is at 15000 Munford Road, Providence Forge, VA. Hours: Mon, Wed, Fri & Sat 7am–5pm; Tue & Thu 9am–7pm; closed Sun and on New Year's Day, Easter, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas (times subject to change without notice). It accepts used motor oil and used antifreeze (residential only, 5 gallons or less; other fluids and oil filters not accepted); lead-acid batteries; household trash; recyclables (cardboard, mixed paper, #1 and #2 narrow-neck plastic bottles, aluminum and steel cans, glass bottles and jars); appliances with liquids drained; push mowers and weed eaters (motors stripped and drained); latex paint only when dried out in the can. Not accepted: oil-based paints and solvents. For tires, CVWMA points residents to tire retailers or the county's annual Tire Amnesty Day; for electronics, to CVWMA electronics recycling events. Fees: No cost to Charles City residents; fees may apply for Freon removal from appliances. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
