Hanover Route 301 Transfer Station

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Richmond, VA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Hanover County Department of Public Works
Full address
7301 Courtland Farm Road, Hanover, VA 23069
Accepted materials
Charged commercial and residential loads: municipal solid waste; electronics; tires; grass, unbagged leaves, brush and logs/limbs up to 12 in. diameter and 6 ft long; loose construction and demolition waste (shingles, roofing, drywall, inert waste, lumber/plywood over 4 ft)
Hours / collection schedule
Mon–Sat 8am–4pm; closed New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day
Fees
Charges apply (call 804-365-3220 for current rates); credit card, debit card or check — no cash. Hanover County residents: no charge for brush up to 4 in. diameter and 6 ft long (low-sided pickup volume) or, with a Public Works permit for oversized vehicles, household waste up to 4,000 lb a year
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Hanover County's transfer station for commercial and charged waste; businesses must use this site rather than the residential convenience centers. 804-365-3220
