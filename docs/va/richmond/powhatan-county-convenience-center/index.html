Powhatan County Convenience Center

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Richmond, VA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Powhatan County Department of Public Works
Full address
2407 Mitchell Road, Powhatan, VA 23139
Accepted materials
Used tires (fee); used motor oil, automatic transmission and power-steering fluid, gear and hydraulic oil, diesel and fuel oil; motor-oil additives; oil filters; antifreeze; gasoline; oil-based paint, varnish/shellac, stains and tints, lacquer thinners, polyurethane, primers, mineral spirits, turpentine and other flammable solvents; lead-acid and rechargeable batteries (no fee); propane tanks; appliances/white goods; household trash; drop-off recycling (cans, bottles, paper, cardboard, cartons); Goodwill donation trailer. Latex paint not accepted for recycling (dry it out and put it in the trash)
Hours / collection schedule
Tue–Sun 8am–6pm; closed New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving (page lists Nov 25–26) and Christmas (Dec 24–25)
Fees
Tires: passenger car/small truck $2, on rims $7, large truck (over 19 in.) $13, large truck on rim $19, tractor $26. Appliances with CFCs $25, without $15. Pickup-load disposal $30 small / $40 regular / $70 larger (up to 10 cu yd); one free pickup load of residential C&D waste per month. No fee for car and rechargeable batteries
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Operated by Powhatan County Public Works. A Powhatan Convenience Center sticker is required as proof of residency (bring vehicle registration showing a Powhatan County address to get one). Commercial waste is not accepted. Fees: https://www.powhatanva.gov/307/Fee-Schedule. Item locations per the CVWMA Powhatan recycling guide: https://cvwma.com/recycling/powhatan/. Electronics: CVWMA says to use reputable recyclers or special events such as the first-Saturday Securis event in Henrico; the county's Recycling page lists electronics among acceptable items, so call before bringing them. 804-598-5673
