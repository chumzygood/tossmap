New Kent County Refuse Site (Route 647, Lanexa)

New Kent County Refuse Site (Route 647, Lanexa) in Lanexa, VA accepts motor oil; fees: No charge for county residents. See hours below. Source: newkent-va.us.

New Kent County Refuse Site (Route 647, Lanexa) is at 12400 Old Telegraph Road, Lanexa, VA 23089. Hours: Tue–Sat 7am–7pm; Sun 10am–7pm; closed Mon (and Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day). It accepts used motor oil, oil filters and antifreeze; household trash; bagged leaves; drop-off recycling (corrugated cardboard, mixed paper and boxes, newspapers, aluminum, glass, plastics #1–7; no plastic bags or Styrofoam). Not accepted: tires, toxic chemicals, gasoline, automobile chassis, concrete and bricks, brush over 6 in. TVs/monitors, lead-acid batteries, paint and appliances are listed only for the Main Refuse Site (Route 618). Fees: No charge for county residents. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
