Dinwiddie Convenience Center

Dinwiddie Convenience Center in Dinwiddie, VA accepts electronics, tires, batteries and motor oil, Mon–Sat 7am–7pm; Sun 1–7pm; fees: Residential waste disposal is free for county residents. Source: dinwiddieva.us.

Dinwiddie Convenience Center is at 13542 Boydton Plank Road, Dinwiddie, VA 23841. Hours: Mon–Sat 7am–7pm; Sun 1–7pm. It accepts household trash; batteries; tires (four per person per day, must be clean; no rims, no equipment or tractor tires); clothing and shoes; metal; used motor oil; electronics; furniture and appliances; recyclables. Not accepted: hazardous materials, propane and other pressurized tanks, dead animals, stumps, campers, boats, trailer loads. Fees: Residential waste disposal is free for county residents. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Eligibility, ID and other notes
Dinwiddie County manned convenience center. The county lists one set of accepted items for its convenience centers; it does not list items center by center. Hazardous materials are not taken by Waste Management; the county's Cooperative Extension Services sponsors an annual disposal event. Construction debris and commercial waste go only to the main center on Wheeler's Pond Road ($45/ton).
