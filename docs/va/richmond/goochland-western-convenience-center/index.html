Goochland Western Convenience Center

Goochland Western Convenience Center in Goochland, VA accepts paint, electronics, tires, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals, Daily 7:30am–6pm (closed county holidays and other announced closures); fees: Free for county residents for household trash and recyclables. Tires: up to 19 in. off rim $2, up to 19 in. on rim $6, 19–24 in. off rim $13, agricultural tires 24.5–42 in. off rim $30 (large loads may be referred to a landfill). Fees also apply to air conditioners, brush and refrigerators/Freon appliances. Source: goochlandva.us.

Accepted materials
Automobile tires (fee; up to 19 in. on or off rim, 19–24 in. off rim, agricultural tires 24.5–42 in. off rim); used motor oil, automatic transmission and power-steering fluid, gear and hydraulic oil, diesel and fuel oil (oil drum); oil filters; antifreeze; leaded/unleaded gasoline, motor-oil additives, oil-based paint, stains, varnish/shellac, polyurethane, primer, lacquer and paint thinners, mineral spirits and turpentine (paint drums); rechargeable and lead-acid batteries; propane tanks; electronics at the permanent electronics trailer (computers, laptops, monitors, printers, keyboards, TVs, stereos, VCRs, cell phones; small consumer appliances, power tools, fans and lamps not accepted in the electronics program); appliances/white goods (Freon recovery); household trash; drop-off recycling (mixed paper, cans, plastic containers #1–7, cartons, glass); Goodwill clothing and shoe box
Hours / collection schedule
Daily 7:30am–6pm (closed county holidays and other announced closures)
Fees
Free for county residents for household trash and recyclables. Tires: up to 19 in. off rim $2, up to 19 in. on rim $6, 19–24 in. off rim $13, agricultural tires 24.5–42 in. off rim $30 (large loads may be referred to a landfill). Fees also apply to air conditioners, brush and refrigerators/Freon appliances
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Operated by Goochland County; county residents only with proof of residency (County Code Sec. 7-71: only county residents may use county-owned waste facilities). Motor oil, paint/solvent and electronics locations per the Central Virginia Waste Management Authority Goochland recycling guide: https://cvwma.com/recycling/goochland/. County Code Sec. 7-71 also lists hazardous waste, solvents and oil among items not to be disposed of at county facilities, so use only the designated oil and paint drums and ask the attendant before unloading. Latex paint: county says dry it out completely and put it in the trash. 804-556-5375

Accepts batteries?Yes — CVWMA Recycling in Goochland guide (https://cvwma.com/recycling/goochland/, fetched 2026-11-21): “Lead acid. Recycle at Goochland County Convenience Centers”; “Rechargeable. Accepted items in the oil drum include: motor oil, automatic transmission fluid, power steering fluid, diesel fuel, gear oil …”; “Oil Filters. Recycle at Goochland County Convenience Centers.”
Accepts household chemicals?Yes — Limited to fuels and paint solvents — CVWMA Recycling in Goochland guide (https://cvwma.com/recycling/goochland/, fetched 2026-11-21): “Recycle leaded and unleaded gasoline along with paint and solvents in the paint drums at Goochland County Convenience Centers”; “mineral spirits, turpentine, other flammable solvents”; “Antifreeze. Recycle at Goochland County convenience centers.” Note Goochland County Solid Waste & Recycling page, County Code Sec. 7-71 (fetched 2026-11-21): “(e) The following items shall not be disposed of at a county-owned facility: stumps, hazardous waste, asbestos, industrial wastes, ash sludge, solvents, slurry, oil …” — ask the attendant.
