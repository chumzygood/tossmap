Prince George County Convenience Center

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Richmond, VA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Meridian Waste for Prince George County (Department of General Services)
Full address
3100 Union Branch Road, South Prince George, VA 23805
Accepted materials
Car and light-duty truck tires (fee; no rims, no farm implement, tractor or commercial tires); electronics (TVs, monitors and terminals carry a fee); used motor oil only (no cooking oil); used antifreeze; fluorescent bulbs; NiCad and automotive batteries; appliances/items containing Freon (fee); mattresses and box springs (fee); household trash (by weight); recycling (glass, plastics, paper, cardboard, cans); yard waste and metals (by weight). Not accepted: propane tanks; oil-based paint and other household hazardous waste (county says to use a professional hazardous waste disposal company); latex paint goes in the trash once dried
Hours / collection schedule
Daily 7am–6pm during Daylight Saving Time (early March–early November); daily 8am–5pm during Eastern Standard Time. Closed New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day; closes at noon New Year's Eve and Christmas Eve
Fees
Recycling: no charge. Trash and debris 6 cents per pound (minimum $3). Tires $10 each (varies by size). Televisions, monitors, terminals $30 each. Items containing Freon $20 each. Mattress or box spring $30 each
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Operated by Meridian Waste for Prince George County. Residents present identification establishing county residency. Battery types per the county Recycling Centers page: https://www.princegeorgecountyva.gov/departments/general_services/convenience_center_-_refuse_recycling/recycling_centers.php. Department of General Services (804) 722-0775; center (804) 733-2795
