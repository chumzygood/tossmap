Hopewell Citizen's Convenience Center

Hopewell Citizen's Convenience Center in Hopewell, VA accepts tires and batteries. See hours below. Source: hopewellva.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Richmond, VA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Hopewell Department of Public Works
Full address
507 Station Street, Hopewell, VA 23860
Accepted materials
Tires without rims (up to 8 per year); residential waste; household furniture; mattresses; car seats; major appliances (washers, dryers, stoves, water heaters); refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners and dehumidifiers (CFC removal fee); brush up to 6 in. diameter, yard and garden waste, leaves and pine needles; propane grill tanks (4–40 lb); rechargeable batteries and cell phones; homeowner-performed construction and demolition debris (building permit required). Not accepted: kitchen or food waste, liquids, paint, petroleum-based products, wet-cell batteries, asbestos and other hazardous products, automobile parts, concrete and masonry, stumps and logs over 6 in.
Hours / collection schedule
Tue, Wed & Fri 12:30–3:30pm; Sat 8am–4:30pm; closed on observed holidays and in inclement weather
Fees
Tires $1 per passenger tire, $2 per light-truck tire; residential waste $3 (car), $10 (pickup/SUV or single-axle trailer), $35 (dual-axle trailer); brush and yard waste free (dual-axle trailer $10); construction debris $3–$50; $10 CFC removal per appliance; $10 extra for vehicles with raised sides
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Operated by the City of Hopewell Department of Public Works, next to the Animal Shelter. City of Hopewell residents only, for waste generated within city limits; businesses and contractors are not allowed. Fees and items from the city's Convenience Center Fees sheet (dated March 2025): https://www.hopewellva.gov/DocumentCenter/View/473/Convenience-Center-Fees-PDF. City of Hopewell 804-541-2200
