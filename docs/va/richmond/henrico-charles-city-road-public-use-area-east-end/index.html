Henrico Charles City Road Public Use Area (East End)

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Richmond, VA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Henrico County
Full address
2075 Charles City Road, Henrico, VA 23231
Accepted materials
Passenger/automotive tires (tire trailer; up to 4 included in trip fee, max 6 per trip); free drop-off of used motor oil, old gasoline, antifreeze and oil-based paints & solvents; lead-acid batteries; recycling; yard waste
Hours / collection schedule
Daily 7:30am–7pm (closed listed holidays)
Fees
$5 per trash/tire trip (includes up to 4 tires; $5 each beyond 4); HHW items (oil, gas, antifreeze, oil-based paint/solvents) free; recycling and yard waste free; $20 per refrigerant appliance
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Henrico County residents only (driver's license with Henrico address); no rental-property, vacant-lot or business waste. Closed New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. (804) 727-8770. HHW program: https://henrico.gov/utility/solid-waste/household-hazardous-waste-disposal/
