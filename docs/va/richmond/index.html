Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Richmond, VA

East Richmond Road Convenience Center

3800 East Richmond Road, Richmond, VA 23223

Accepts: Paints (20 gal/month/household), solvents, thinners, stains, varnish, motor oil, antifreeze, car fluids, lead-acid car batteries, cooking oil, fuels, propane tanks
Hours: Mon–Fri 7am–3:30pm; Sat 8:30am–2pm; closed Sun & holidays
Fees: Free
Notes: Richmond residents with proof of residency; no pesticides, herbicides, acetone, fire extinguishers
