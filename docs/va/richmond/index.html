Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Richmond, VA

East Richmond Road Convenience Center

3800 East Richmond Road, Richmond, VA 23223

Accepts: Paints (20 gal/month/household), solvents, thinners, stains, varnish, motor oil, antifreeze, car fluids, lead-acid car batteries, cooking oil, fuels; passenger tires without rims (limit 4; no off-road or oversized)
Hours: Mon–Fri 7am–3:30pm; Sat 8:30am–2pm; closed Sun & holidays
Fees: Free
Notes: Richmond residents with proof of residency (license, utility bill, or registration). No pesticides, herbicides, fire extinguishers, asbestos, explosives. 804-646-4706

Richmond Southside Transfer Station

3520 North Hopkins Road, Richmond, VA

Accepts: Passenger tires without rims (limit 4 free; $1 each beyond 4; tractor-trailer tires $5 each); large bulk items, appliances, brush/yard waste; glass, cardboard, aluminum cans, newspapers
Hours: Mon–Fri 6:30am–4:30pm; Sat 8:30am–2pm; closed Sun & holidays
Fees: Tires: 4 free then $1 each (passenger); tractor-trailer $5 each
Notes: City residents; no commercial/contractor debris. 804-232-8488

Henrico Springfield Road Public Use Area (West End)

10600 Fords Country Lane, Glen Allen, VA 23059

Accepts: Passenger/automotive tires (tire trailer; up to 4 included in trip fee, max 6 per trip); free drop-off of used motor oil, old gasoline, antifreeze and oil-based paints & solvents; lead-acid batteries; recycling; yard waste
Hours: Daily 7:30am–7pm (closed listed holidays)
Fees: $5 per trash/tire trip (includes up to 4 tires; $5 each beyond 4); HHW items (oil, gas, antifreeze, oil-based paint/solvents) free; recycling and yard waste free; $20 per refrigerant appliance
Notes: Henrico County residents only (driver's license with Henrico address); no rental-property, vacant-lot or business waste. Closed New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. (804) 727-8770. HHW program: https://henrico.gov/utility/solid-waste/household-hazardous-waste-disposal/
