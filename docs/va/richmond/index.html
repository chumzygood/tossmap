Where can I drop off paint in Richmond?

East Richmond Road Convenience Center at 3800 East Richmond Road, Richmond, VA 23223 is listed as accepting paint. The listing records acceptance as: Paints (20 gal/month/household), solvents, thinners, stains, varnish, motor oil, antifreeze, car fluids, lead-acid car batteries, cooking oil, fuels; passenger tires without rims (limit 4; no off-road or oversized). Richmond residents with proof of residency (license, utility bill, or registration). No pesticides, herbicides, fire extinguishers, asbestos, explosives. 804-646-4706. Henrico Springfield Road Public Use Area (West End) and Henrico Charles City Road Public Use Area (East End) are listed as accepting paint. The listing records acceptance as: Passenger/automotive tires (tire trailer; up to 4 included in trip fee, max 6 per trip); free drop-off of used motor oil, old gasoline, antifreeze and oil-based paints & solvents; lead-acid batteries; recycling; yard waste. Henrico County residents only (driver's license with Henrico address); no rental-property, vacant-lot or business waste. Closed New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. (804) 727-8770. HHW program: https://henrico.gov/utility/solid-waste/household-hazardous-waste-disposal/. Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.
