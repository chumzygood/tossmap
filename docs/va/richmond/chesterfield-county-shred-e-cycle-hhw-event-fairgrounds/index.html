Chesterfield County Shred, E-Cycle & HHW Recycling Event (Fairgrounds)

Chesterfield County Shred, E-Cycle & HHW Recycling Event (Fairgrounds) in Chesterfield, VA accepts paint, electronics, batteries and household chemicals. See hours below. Source: cvwma.com.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Richmond, VA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Chesterfield County
Full address
Chesterfield County Fairgrounds, 10300 Courthouse Road, Chesterfield, VA
Accepted materials
Household hazardous waste (no fee): acids, drain cleaner, bleach, ammonia, paint thinner, lighter fluid, brake fluid, pool chemicals, oil-based paint (limit 5 gallons), rechargeable batteries (limit 5), fertilizers, pesticides/herbicides, thermostats/thermometers, aerosol cans (limit 10), fluorescent tubes, CFL and LED bulbs (limit 6 each), 3D printing chemicals. Electronics (fees) and paper shredding (up to 5 small boxes or bags free)
Hours / collection schedule
Event: Sat Mar 13, 2027, 9am–noon. Next listed date: Sat Oct 30, 2027, 9am–noon
Fees
HHW and paper shredding free; CRT TVs $28 (27 inch or less) or $37 (over 27 inch); hard drive, phone and tablet shredding $8–$10.50 per item; other electronics $5.25–$40 per item
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Chesterfield County recycling event for county residents, listed on the CVWMA events calendar (Oct 30, 2027 event: https://cvwma.com/event/chesterfield-county-shred-e-cycle-and-household-household-waste-recycling-event/). Limits: acids, bases and solvents under 5 gallons each; pool chemicals 2 bags or 5 containers, under 3 gallons; pesticides/herbicides 5 containers, under 3 gallons; fertilizers 2 bags; thermostats/thermometers 2
