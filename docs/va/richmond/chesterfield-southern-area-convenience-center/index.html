Chesterfield Southern Area Convenience Center

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Richmond, VA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Chesterfield County
Full address
6700 Landfill Drive, Chester, VA 23831
Accepted materials
Tires (limit 5; oversized tires not accepted); used motor oil, antifreeze and residential oil filters (10 gal per visit); electronics and TVs (go in the trash containers for a $9 fee; recycle them at county e-cycling events instead); 1 lb and 20 lb propane tanks (limit 5); appliances; household trash; recyclables. NOT accepted: oil-based paint (county events only), fire extinguishers, propane tanks over 30 lb
Hours / collection schedule
Daily 7am–6pm (closed listed county holidays)
Fees
Tires $2 each off-rim, $5 each on-rim; used oil and antifreeze free; electronics/TVs $9; trash or yard debris $9 per pickup load; Freon appliances $15, other appliances $10
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Chesterfield County residents; be prepared to show ID/proof of county residency. Closed Veterans Day (Nov 11), Thanksgiving and the day after (Nov 26–27), Christmas Eve and Day; closes at noon Dec 31. Card or cash ($1–$50 bills). 804-748-1297
