MMSD South Drop Off Center

Milwaukee residents can drop off paint, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals at MMSD South Drop Off Center. See hours below. Source: mmsd.com.

MMSD South Drop Off Center is at 3879 W Lincoln Ave, Milwaukee, WI 53215. Hours: Thu–Sat 7am–3pm; 2026 closed days: Jan. 1, Apr. 3, Jun. 19, July 4, Nov. 26, Nov. 27, Dec. 24, Dec. 25, and Jan. 1, 2027. It accepts household hazardous waste from Milwaukee County homes and apartments, including automotive fluids (antifreeze, brake fluid, gasoline, oil and oil filters, transmission fluid and related products); latex paint (limit 20 gallons per visit) and non-latex paint (no limit); paint thinner, solvents, adhesives, driveway sealer and varnish; household cleaners, pesticides, pool chemicals, fertilizers and mercury thermometers and thermostats; button, lithium-ion, rechargeable and reusable batteries (car batteries are not accepted); fluorescent tubes (limit 20 per visit), CFLs, LEDs and holiday lights; and 1-lb and 20-lb propane tanks at no additional charge. Not accepted: ammunition, compressed gas cylinders, car batteries, clothes dryers, computers, containers larger than 15 gallons, explosives, fire extinguishers, fireworks, helium tanks, marine flares, medical waste, microwaves, oxygen tanks, prescription drugs, radioactive wastes, refrigerators, sharps/needles, smoke detectors, stereos, televisions, or tires of any type. Asbestos is accepted only at the Menomonee Falls collection center (limit two contractor-sized bags, triple bagged), not at this site. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Eligibility, ID and other notes
Milwaukee County households only. No businesses, contractors, schools, churches, non-profits, or government agencies. Stay in your vehicle; put items in the trunk or as close to the hatchback as possible. Gas cans used to transport old gas or gas/oil mixtures are returned. Phone (414) 225-2066.
