North Transfer Station (Seattle Public Utilities)

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Seattle, WA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Seattle Public Utilities
Full address
1350 North 34th Street, Seattle, WA 98103
Accepted materials
Vehicle tires; vehicle batteries and scrap metal (recycling); household garbage and larger items; yard waste; appliances (no commercial appliances); household sharps. NOT accepted: electronics, TVs or monitors (CRTs); hazardous waste, oil paint and household chemicals; sealed drums, fuel/propane/oil tanks, compressed gas, fire extinguishers; asbestos; material over 8 ft long and over 200 lb
Hours / collection schedule
Daily 8am–5:30pm (first Wednesday of the month 10am–5:30pm); closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day and July 4
Fees
Fees apply (see Seattle Public Utilities transfer station rates); cash, check, Visa or MasterCard; checks require a valid ID
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Seattle Public Utilities transfer station. Loads must be secured or may incur a fee. Opens 10am on the first Wednesday of the month. SPU directs household chemicals and other hazardous items to Household Hazardous Waste facilities. Rates, hours and accepted materials: (206) 684-8400
