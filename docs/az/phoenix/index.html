Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Phoenix, AZ

Phoenix has no public HHW drop-off; residential customers get one at-home HHW collection per year. The 27th Ave Transfer Station does not accept HHW. Source: https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/residential-trash-recycling/household-hazardous-waste-collection.html

North Gateway Transfer Station

30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy., Phoenix, AZ 85085

Accepts: Passenger car tires (5 per year, rims removed; no off-road, semi or motorcycle tires), appliances, CRT and non-CRT TVs (2 CRTs/month free), household trash
Hours: Mon–Fri 5:30am–5pm; Sat 6am–3pm; closed Sun & city holidays (closed Wed Nov 11, 2026)
Fees: Residents: one load up to 1 ton/month free with city services bill + matching photo ID; otherwise $55/ton ($40 min); extra CRTs $20 each
Notes: Household hazardous waste is prohibited
