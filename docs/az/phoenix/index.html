Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Phoenix, AZ

Phoenix has no public HHW drop-off; residential customers get one at-home HHW collection per year. The 27th Ave Transfer Station does not accept HHW. Source: https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/residential-trash-recycling/household-hazardous-waste-collection.html

No permanent drop-off site found in official sources.
