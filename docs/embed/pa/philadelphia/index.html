Where to drop off hazardous waste in Philadelphia, PA

Sanitation Convenience Center – Port Richmond3901 Delaware Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19137
Accepts: Automotive tires (limit 4 per day); electronic waste including computers, monitors and televisions; latex or water-based paint (must be solidified with an absorbent such as kitty litter or newspaper); fluorescent light bulbs; lithium, rechargeable and lead-acid batteries; bulk items, large metal household items and appliances incl. items with refrigerants (limit 2 per day); mattresses and box springs (unwrapped); trash (up to 6 receptacles or 12 bags); recyclables; yard waste (paper bags only); Christmas trees. NOT accepted: construction debris
Hours: Mon–Sat 8am–6pm (closed City holidays)
