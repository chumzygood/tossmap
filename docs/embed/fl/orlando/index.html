Orange County Landfill (HHW, electronics & tires)5901 Young Pine Road, Orlando, FL 32829
Accepts: Household hazardous waste free for Orange County residents (paint products, pesticides, propane tanks up to 100 lb, cooking oil, fluorescent bulbs, car fluids, rechargeable batteries, household cleaners, pool chemicals) and electronics with a circuit board (phones, computers, tablets, TVs except rear-projection, VCRs, CD players, stereos); waste passenger tires (up to 8 per load); garbage, C&D debris, yard waste; freon appliances. Not accepted at HHW drop-off: tires, rear-projection TVs, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, flares, prescription drugs
Hours: Mon–Sat 8am–5pm (household hazardous waste Mon–Sat 8am–5pm; bulk tire loads Mon–Fri 8am–4pm); closed Thanksgiving and Christmas
