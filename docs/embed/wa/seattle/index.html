Where to drop off hazardous waste in Seattle, WA

North Seattle HHW Facility12550 Stone Ave N, Seattle, WA 98133
Accepts: Adhesives and caulks, antifreeze, automotive products, batteries (lead-acid, alkaline, button, rechargeable), fluorescent lights, gasoline, household cleaners, lawn and garden products, marine flares, mercury products, motor oil, oil-based paints and stains, paint thinner and other solvents, pesticides, pool and spa chemicals, and propane and butane tanks (up to 8 gallons, 5 items max). Not accepted: appliances, asbestos-containing materials, electronics, TVs and computers, empty containers, electric vehicle batteries, explosives, medical sharps, medicines, smoke detectors, Styrofoam, tires, or latex paint (King County directs latex paint to PaintCare drop-off sites).
Hours: Sun–Tue 9am–5pm; closed July 4, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year’s Day

North Transfer Station (Seattle Public Utilities)1350 North 34th Street, Seattle, WA 98103
Accepts: Vehicle tires; vehicle batteries and scrap metal (recycling); household garbage and larger items; yard waste; appliances (no commercial appliances); household sharps. NOT accepted: electronics, TVs or monitors (CRTs); hazardous waste, oil paint and household chemicals; sealed drums, fuel/propane/oil tanks, compressed gas, fire extinguishers; asbestos; material over 8 ft long and over 200 lb
Hours: Daily 8am–5:30pm (first Wednesday of the month 10am–5:30pm); closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day and July 4
