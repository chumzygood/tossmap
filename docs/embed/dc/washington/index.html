Accepts: Household hazardous waste: aerosols, antifreeze and automotive fluids, gasoline, motor oil, rechargeable batteries, cooking oil, pool chemicals, household cleaners, lawn and garden chemicals and pesticides, oil-based paint, paint thinners, stains and varnish, mercury devices, CFLs and fluorescent tubes, propane tanks up to 20 lb; e-waste (TVs, computers, gaming systems, keyboards, speakers, cords); separated recycling incl. used motor oil, antifreeze and automobile batteries; bicycles; textiles; household trash.
