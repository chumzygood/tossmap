Where to drop off hazardous waste in Minneapolis, MN

Hennepin County Drop-off – Brooklyn Park8100 Jefferson Hwy, Brooklyn Park, MN
Accepts: Household hazardous waste and problem materials including paint, stain, varnish, solvents and driveway sealer (five-gallon pails limited to three per year); pesticides; fluorescent bulbs (limit 25 per year); aerosols; pool chemicals; mercury thermometers and thermostats; oil filters; automotive fluids; motor oil in containers of 5 gallons or less; gas, fuels and other fluids in containers of 5 gallons or less (cans are left at the facility); batteries of all types (bag household batteries or tape the terminals); needles and sharps (follow the county’s preparation guide); electronics (computer peripherals, audio and video equipment, phones and rechargeable products; TVs, monitors and laptops for a fee; limit of five of the same electronics type per year); tires for a fee (under 4 feet, no bicycle tires). Recycling and organics are also accepted under county rules. Mattresses and box springs are accepted at the Brooklyn Park Transfer Station only. Not accepted: garbage, ammunition, asbestos, business hazardous waste, carpet, concrete or asphalt, construction materials, empty gas cans, explosives, furniture, infectious waste, medicines, non-reusable textiles, remodeling debris, wood, or yard and tree waste.
Hours: Tue–Sat 9am–5pm; closed Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year’s Day
