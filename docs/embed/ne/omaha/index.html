Where to drop off hazardous waste in Omaha, NE

Under The Sink4001 S 120th St, Omaha, NE 68137
Accepts: Household hazardous waste: latex and oil-based paint, spray paint, paint thinner and remover, stains and varnish; pesticides, weed killers and garden chemicals; pool chemicals; cleaning products, drain and oven cleaners; motor oil, oil filters, antifreeze, gasoline and automotive fluids; automotive batteries, lithium and rechargeable batteries; fluorescent tubes and CFLs; mercury devices; propane bottles up to 1 lb; sharps in a rigid labeled container. Not accepted: business waste, single-use (alkaline/button) batteries, electronics, tires, appliances, medications, fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, propane tanks over 1 lb
Hours: Wed 9am–4:45pm; Thu 9am–6:15pm; Fri 9am–4:45pm; Sat by appointment
