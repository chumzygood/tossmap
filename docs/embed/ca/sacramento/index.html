Kiefer Landfill (tires, e-waste & ABOP drop-off)12701 Kiefer Blvd., Sloughhouse, CA 95683
Accepts: Free residential drop-off: passenger/light-truck tires (rims OK, 4 per day free), e-waste (TVs, CRTs, monitors, computers, laptops, tablets, phones, printers, gaming consoles, stereos), large appliances (2 per day free), small corded appliances and power tools, mattresses and box springs, clean metal, clean inerts. ABOP & Special Waste facility (residents): antifreeze, household and car batteries, motor oil and filters, cooking oil, latex and oil-based paint, aerosol cans, brake/transmission fluid, fluorescent tubes and bulbs (12 tubes per month), home-generated sharps in sealed containers, mercury thermometers and switches, barbecue propane tanks (limit two). Not accepted at ABOP: acids, household cleaners, pesticides and herbicides, solvents, paint thinners, gasoline, pool chemicals, ammunition, explosives, medical waste. Products with built-in batteries go to the e-waste area. Also takes household trash, green waste and treated wood (fees)
Hours: Landfill: Mon–Fri 6:30am–4:30pm; Sat–Sun 8:30am–4:30pm (arrive at least 15 minutes before closing; may close early if the daily vehicle limit is reached). Thanksgiving Day closes at 2pm; Christmas and New Year's Day close at 2pm on weekdays, all day on weekends. ABOP & Special Waste drop-off: Tue–Sat 8:30am–4pm (closed Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day)

Elk Grove Special Waste Collection Center9255 Disposal Lane, Elk Grove, CA 95624
Accepts: Household hazardous waste: latex and oil-based paint, paint thinner and stripper, varnish; motor oil, antifreeze, transmission fluid, gasoline and diesel; auto and motorcycle batteries and household batteries; fluorescent and LED bulbs, mercury devices; pesticides, herbicides and fertilizer; pool chemicals; household cleaners; aerosol cans; cooking oil and grease; propane cylinders (5 gal or less); household fire extinguishers; smoke alarms; over-the-counter medicines; sharps in rigid containers; non-friable asbestos (special packaging, max 125 lb). Electronics: TVs (CRT, LCD, plasma), computers, laptops, monitors, tablets, cell phones, telephones, printers, copiers, fax machines, radios and stereos, VCR/DVD players. Not accepted: trash, mattresses, Styrofoam (EPS), explosives and ammunition, infectious medical waste, large compressed gas cylinders, radioactive waste, treated wood, prescription (controlled) medications
Hours: Residents: Sat–Wed 9am–4pm. Reuse Room: Sat–Wed 9am–3:30pm (Elk Grove residents only). Small businesses: Wednesdays by appointment only. Closed all City holidays, including Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day

City of Sacramento HHW Facility (Sacramento Recycling and Transfer Station)8491 Fruitridge Road, Sacramento, CA 95826
Accepts: Household hazardous waste: latex and oil-based paint, paint thinners, solvents; used motor oil and filters, antifreeze, brake/transmission fluid, automotive products, gasoline and other flammables; car batteries and household batteries; fluorescent lamps and tubes, mercury thermometers; pesticides and herbicides; pool chemicals; household cleaners; acids and bases; aerosol cans; kitchen oils; propane cylinders; flares; vape pens and e-cigarettes; sharps (special packaging); electronic waste (anything with a plug or that runs on batteries). Not accepted: air bags, ammunition, asbestos, compressed gas cylinders (except propane and handheld aerosols), explosives and fireworks, infectious and medical waste (except sharps), medications, radioactive and reactive waste, treated wood
Hours: Tue–Sat 8am–5pm
