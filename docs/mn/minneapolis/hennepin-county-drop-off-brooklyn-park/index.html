Hennepin County Drop-off – Brooklyn Park

Minneapolis residents can drop off paint, electronics, tires, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals at Hennepin County Drop-off – Brooklyn Park. See hours below. Source: hennepincounty.gov.

Hennepin County Drop-off – Brooklyn Park is at 8100 Jefferson Hwy, Brooklyn Park, MN. Hours: Tue–Sat 9am–5pm; closed Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year’s Day. It accepts household hazardous waste and problem materials including paint, stain, varnish, solvents and driveway sealer (five-gallon pails limited to three per year); pesticides; fluorescent bulbs (limit 25 per year); aerosols; pool chemicals; mercury thermometers and thermostats; oil filters; automotive fluids; motor oil in containers of 5 gallons or less; gas, fuels and other fluids in containers of 5 gallons or less (cans are left at the facility); batteries of all types (bag household batteries or tape the terminals); needles and sharps (follow the county’s preparation guide); electronics (computer peripherals, audio and video equipment, phones and rechargeable products; TVs, monitors and laptops for a fee; limit of five of the same electronics type per year); tires for a fee (under 4 feet, no bicycle tires). Recycling and organics are also accepted under county rules. Mattresses and box springs are accepted at the Brooklyn Park Transfer Station only. Not accepted: garbage, ammunition, asbestos, business hazardous waste, carpet, concrete or asphalt, construction materials, empty gas cans, explosives, furniture, infectious waste, medicines, non-reusable textiles, remodeling debris, wood, or yard and tree waste. Fees: Tires $5 each (with or without rims, less than 4 feet in diameter, no bicycle tires; up to 16 per load and 32 per household per year); TVs, computer monitors and laptops $10 each; large appliances $15 each. Mattresses and box springs are $25 each at the Brooklyn Park Transfer Station only. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Eligibility, ID and other notes
HHW is accepted from households in Hennepin, Anoka, Carver, Dakota, Ramsey, Scott and Washington counties; residents of Sherburne and Wright counties are not eligible. Proof of residency (such as a driver’s license or utility bill) is required. Transport HHW in sealed, labeled containers of 5 gallons or less, upright in a plastic-lined box. Residents should remain in their vehicles unless staff directs otherwise. Call 612-348-3777 or check the Green Disposal Guide before you go. A Free Product Center is on site for usable leftover products.
