Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.

8100 Jefferson Hwy, Brooklyn Park, MN

Accepts: Household hazardous waste and problem materials including paint, stain, varnish, solvents and driveway sealer (five-gallon pails limited to three per year); pesticides; fluorescent bulbs (limit 25 per year); aerosols; pool chemicals; mercury thermometers and thermostats; oil filters; automotive fluids; motor oil in containers of 5 gallons or less; gas, fuels and other fluids in containers of 5 gallons or less (cans are left at the facility); batteries of all types (bag household batteries or tape the terminals); needles and sharps (follow the county’s preparation guide); electronics (computer peripherals, audio and video equipment, phones and rechargeable products; TVs, monitors and laptops for a fee; limit of five of the same electronics type per year); tires for a fee (under 4 feet, no bicycle tires). Not accepted: garbage, ammunition, asbestos, business hazardous waste, carpet, concrete or asphalt, construction materials, empty gas cans, explosives, furniture, infectious waste, medicines, non-reusable textiles, remodeling debris, wood, or yard and tree waste.
Hours: Tue–Sat 9am–5pm; closed Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year’s Day
Fees: Tires $5 each (with or without rims, less than 4 feet in diameter, no bicycle tires; up to 16 per load and 32 per household per year); TVs, computer monitors and laptops $10 each; large appliances $15 each. Mattresses and box springs are $25 each at the Brooklyn Park Transfer Station only.
Notes: HHW is accepted from households in Hennepin, Anoka, Carver, Dakota, Ramsey, Scott and Washington counties; residents of Sherburne and Wright counties are not eligible. Proof of residency (such as a driver’s license or utility bill) is required. Transport HHW in sealed, labeled containers of 5 gallons or less, upright in a plastic-lined box. Notes: HHW is accepted from households in Hennepin, Anoka, Carver, Dakota, Ramsey, Scott and Washington counties; residents of Sherburne and Wright counties are not eligible. Proof of residency (such as a driver’s license or utility bill) is required. Transport HHW in sealed, labeled containers of 5 gallons or less, upright in a plastic-lined box. Residents should remain in their vehicles unless staff directs otherwise. Cardboard at this site is limited to one pickup-truck load per visit, in pieces 3 feet by 3 feet or smaller. Call 612-348-3777 or check the Green Disposal Guide before you go. A Free Product Center is on site; as of the official page, the Bloomington reuse shop was temporarily closed for construction beginning May 18, while the drop-off facility remains open.
