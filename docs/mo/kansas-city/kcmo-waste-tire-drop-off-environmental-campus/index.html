KCMO Waste Tire Drop-Off (Environmental Campus)

KCMO Waste Tire Drop-Off (Environmental Campus) in Kansas City, MO accepts tires. See hours below. Source: kcmo.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Kansas City, MO. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Kansas City, MO
Full address
4707 Deramus Ave., Kansas City, MO 64120
Accepted materials
Waste tires, rimmed or rimless: car, truck, tractor, lawnmower and bike tires; rims without tires
Hours / collection schedule
First Saturday of each month, March through November (except holidays), 8am–noon. No December–February drop-off days
Fees
Cash only. Car tire $1.50 rimless / $3.00 on rim; truck tire $4.00 / $8.00; tractor tire $11.00 / $17.00; rim without tire $0.50; lawnmower or bike tire $0.50; $2.00 extra per muddy tire. Neighborhood groups with an approved letter drop off at no charge
Eligibility, ID and other notes
City of Kansas City, MO residents only; proof of residence required. Not intended for businesses. Tires are not collected with residential trash. Questions: Arthur.Baker@kcmo.org. KCMO 311: 816-513-1313
