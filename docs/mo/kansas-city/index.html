Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.

KCMO Waste Tire Drop-Off (Environmental Campus)

4707 Deramus Ave., Kansas City, MO 64120

Accepts: Waste tires, rimmed or rimless: car, truck, tractor, lawnmower and bike tires; rims without tires
Hours: First Saturday of each month, March through November (except holidays), 8am–noon. No December–February drop-off days
Fees: Cash only. Car tire $1.50 rimless / $3.00 on rim; truck tire $4.00 / $8.00; tractor tire $11.00 / $17.00; rim without tire $0.50; lawnmower or bike tire $0.50; $2.00 extra per muddy tire. Neighborhood groups with an approved letter drop off at no charge
Notes: City of Kansas City, MO residents only; proof of residence required. Not intended for businesses. Tires are not collected with residential trash. Questions: Arthur.Baker@kcmo.org. KCMO 311: 816-513-1313
