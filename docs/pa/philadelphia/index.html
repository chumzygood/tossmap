Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.

Accepts: Automotive tires (limit 4 per day); electronic waste including computers, monitors and televisions; latex or water-based paint (must be solidified with an absorbent such as kitty litter or newspaper); fluorescent light bulbs; lithium, rechargeable and lead-acid batteries; bulk items, large metal household items and appliances incl. items with refrigerants (limit 2 per day); mattresses and box springs (unwrapped); trash (up to 6 receptacles or 12 bags); recyclables; yard waste (paper bags only); Christmas trees. NOT accepted: construction debris
Hours: Mon–Sat 8am–6pm (closed City holidays)
Fees: Free (Streets Department: “there is no charge to drop-off materials”)
Notes: Philadelphia residents only; present proof that you live in Philadelphia. Regular household items once a day, oversized items once a week. Personal or commercial vehicles under 6,000 lb total vehicle weight; commercial contractors and commercial loads not allowed. Closed City holidays. Address and phone from the City’s Recycling and Donation Finder; (215) 685-3955
