Sanitation Convenience Center – Northwest Philadelphia

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Philadelphia, PA. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of Philadelphia
Full address
320 Domino Ln, Philadelphia, PA 19128
Accepted materials
Automotive tires (limit 4 per day); electronic waste including computers, monitors and televisions; latex or water-based paint (must be solidified with an absorbent such as kitty litter or newspaper); fluorescent light bulbs; lithium, rechargeable and lead-acid batteries; bulk items, large metal household items and appliances incl. items with refrigerants (limit 2 per day); mattresses and box springs (unwrapped); trash (up to 6 receptacles or 12 bags); recyclables; yard waste (paper bags only); Christmas trees. NOT accepted: construction debris
Hours / collection schedule
Mon–Sat 8am–6pm (closed City holidays)
Fees
Free (Streets Department: “there is no charge to drop-off materials”)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Philadelphia residents only; present proof that you live in Philadelphia. Regular household items once a day, oversized items once a week. Personal or commercial vehicles under 6,000 lb total vehicle weight; commercial contractors and commercial loads not allowed. Closed City holidays. Address and phone from the City’s Recycling and Donation Finder; (215) 685-2502
