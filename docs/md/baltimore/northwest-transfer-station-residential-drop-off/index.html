Northwest Transfer Station (residential drop-off)

Baltimore residents can drop off electronics, tires and motor oil at Northwest Transfer Station (residential drop-off), Mon–Sat 7am–5pm. Source: baltimorecity.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Baltimore, MD. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Baltimore City Department of Public Works
Full address
5030 Reisterstown Road, Baltimore, MD
Accepted materials
Tires without rims; electronics; used motor oil (up to 5 quarts); household trash; furniture and bulk items; appliances; scrap metal; recyclables (cans, glass, plastics, paper, cardboard); bagged yard waste. NOT accepted: paint, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, acids, caustics, gasoline, mercury thermometers/thermostats, syringes, fire extinguishers, propane and helium tanks, batteries, fluorescent bulbs
Hours / collection schedule
Mon–Sat 7am–5pm
Fees
Free for Baltimore City residents; permitted small haulers: $20 flat fee for vehicles up to 7,000 lb gross weight, plus $3.38 per 100 lb beyond 7,000 lb (cash or credit/debit)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Baltimore City residents with valid ID only; a resident who is not a small hauler is limited to 2 loads per day and 5 per week; passenger car, unmodified van/minivan or unmodified pickup up to 3/4 ton only (commercial vehicles, modified pickups and trailers need a Baltimore City small haulers permit). Accepted-items guide: https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/trash-recycling/drop-off-center-guide. Small haulers with a valid permit may also use this site. 410-396-2706
