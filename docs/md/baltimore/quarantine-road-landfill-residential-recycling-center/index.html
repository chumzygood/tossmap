Quarantine Road Landfill & Residential Recycling Center

Quarantine Road Landfill & Residential Recycling Center in Baltimore, MD accepts electronics, tires and motor oil, Mon–Sat 9am–3:30pm (small/large haulers and landfill use: Mon–Sat 9am–3:30pm), Free for Baltimore City residents dropping off recycling, electronics, appliances and household waste. Effective Nov 1, 2025, $135 per ton for commercial haulers and for residents disposing of non-hazardous solid waste at the landfill; residential C&D accepted only here, at the scale house, for $135 per ton. Source: https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/trash-recycling/recycle-drop-off-centers.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Baltimore, MD. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Baltimore City Department of Public Works
Full address
6100 Quarantine Road, Baltimore, MD
Accepted materials
Tires without rims; electronics; used motor oil (up to 5 quarts); household trash; furniture and bulk items; appliances; scrap metal; recyclables (cans, glass, plastics, paper, cardboard); bagged yard waste. NOT accepted: paint, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, acids, caustics, gasoline, mercury thermometers/thermostats, syringes, fire extinguishers, propane and helium tanks, batteries, fluorescent bulbs
Hours / collection schedule
Mon–Sat 9am–3:30pm (small/large haulers and landfill use: Mon–Sat 9am–3:30pm)
Fees
Free for Baltimore City residents dropping off recycling, electronics, appliances and household waste. Effective Nov 1, 2025, $135 per ton for commercial haulers and for residents disposing of non-hazardous solid waste at the landfill; residential C&D accepted only here, at the scale house, for $135 per ton
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Baltimore City residents with valid ID only; a resident who is not a small hauler is limited to 2 loads per day and 5 per week; passenger car, unmodified van/minivan or unmodified pickup up to 3/4 ton only (commercial vehicles, modified pickups and trailers need a Baltimore City small haulers permit). Accepted-items guide: https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/trash-recycling/drop-off-center-guide. Small haulers with a valid permit may use this site, and all large haulers must use it. Does not accept hazardous C&D materials, lead-based or asbestos-containing materials, or free-flowing liquids such as wet paint. 410-396-3772
