Baltimore County Eastern Sanitary Landfill Drop-Off Center (White Marsh)

Baltimore County Eastern Sanitary Landfill Drop-Off Center (White Marsh) in White Marsh, MD accepts electronics, tires, batteries and motor oil, Mon–Sat 7:30am–3:30pm (no admittance after 3:15pm). Source: baltimorecountymd.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Baltimore, MD. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Baltimore County Bureau of Solid Waste Management
Full address
6259 Days Cove Road, White Marsh, MD 21162
Accepted materials
Tires: up to four clean passenger tires, off rims (no racing slicks); electronics (PCs, VCRs, phones, TVs, monitors; residential only); used motor oil, diesel, heating and hydraulic oil (max 25 gallons, no 55-gallon drums); antifreeze; lead-acid (auto, boat) and rechargeable household/tool batteries; propane tanks up to 20 lb; mercury thermometers and thermostats; fluorescent bulbs (10 per visit); scrap metal; single-stream recycling; household trash; yard materials; gasoline (max 25 gallons). Not accepted here: paint, household chemicals/solvents and pesticides (take those to the Central Acceptance Facility in Cockeysville)
Hours / collection schedule
Mon–Sat 7:30am–3:30pm (no admittance after 3:15pm)
Fees
In most cases free for Baltimore County residents, up to 10 visits per year; after 10 visits, materials must go to the Eastern Sanitary Landfill at a prorated $125 per ton. Commercial haulers need a Baltimore County commercial account (MSW $125/ton, special handling $200/ton, organics and brush $65/ton); non-account holders pay a $10 minimum per transaction
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Operated by the Baltimore County Bureau of Solid Waste Management (Baltimore County, Maryland — separate from Baltimore City) for county residents; commercial haulers need a county commercial account. Item-by-facility rules: https://www.baltimorecountymd.gov/departments/public-works/solid-waste/hhw/accepted-materials. Customer service for all three county drop-off centers: 410-887-2000
