Where can I drop off paint in Baltimore?

Sisson Street Residential Recycling Center (HHW days) at 2840 Sisson St, Baltimore, MD 21211 is listed as accepting paint. Recorded hours: Collection days: Dec 4–5, 2026, 9am–5pm (Nov 6–7 already held). This is a scheduled event, not a permanent drop-off site. Baltimore City residents only; materials in original labeled packaging; no commercial vehicles. 410-396-7250. Baltimore County Central Acceptance Facility (Cockeysville) at 201 West Warren Road, Cockeysville, MD 21030 is listed as accepting paint. The listing records acceptance as: Tires: up to four clean passenger tires, off rims (no racing slicks); electronics (PCs, VCRs, phones, TVs, monitors; residential only); used motor oil, diesel, heating and hydraulic oil (max 25 gallons, no 55-gallon drums); antifreeze; lead-acid (auto, boat) and rechargeable household/tool batteries; propane tanks up to 20 lb; mercury thermometers and thermostats; fluorescent bulbs (10 per visit); scrap metal; single-stream recycling; household trash; yard materials; gasoline (max 25 gallons); household hazardous waste: latex and oil paint (max 20 gallons per visit, residential only), acids, chemicals and solvents, pesticides and herbicides (residential only), cooking oil, oil filters. Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.
