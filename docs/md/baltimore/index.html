Where can I drop off electronics in Baltimore?

Eastern Residential Recycling Center at 6101 Bowley's Lane, Baltimore, MD is listed as accepting electronics. The listing records acceptance as: Tires without rims; electronics; used motor oil (up to 5 quarts); household trash; furniture and bulk items; appliances; scrap metal; recyclables (cans, glass, plastics, paper, cardboard); bagged yard waste. NOT accepted: paint, solvents, pesticides, pool chemicals, acids, caustics, gasoline, mercury thermometers/thermostats, syringes, fire extinguishers, propane and helium tanks, batteries, fluorescent bulbs. Baltimore City residents with valid ID only; a resident who is not a small hauler is limited to 2 loads per day and 5 per week; passenger car, unmodified van/minivan or unmodified pickup up to 3/4 ton only (commercial vehicles, modified pickups and trailers need a Baltimore City small haulers permit). Do not treat an event date as year-round hours. Confirm both options on the official source before you go. Last checked 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-18. Sources: https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/recycling-services/household-hazardous-waste-recycling; https://www.baltimorecity.gov/publicworks/trash-recycling/recycle-drop-off-centers.

Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.
