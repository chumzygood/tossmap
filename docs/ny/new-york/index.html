DSNY Special Waste Drop-Off – Bronx

Farragut St & East River, Hunts Point, Bronx, NY

Accepts: Aerosols, batteries, e-waste, vapes, fluorescent bulbs/CFLs, motor oil (10 qt), oil filters (2), paint (5 gal), passenger tires (4), mercury devices (2)
Hours: Tue–Sat 9am–3pm
Fees: Free
Notes: NYC residents only; proof of residency required
