Wimauma Solid Waste Facility (Community Collection Center)

Tampa residents can drop off paint, electronics, tires and batteries at Wimauma Solid Waste Facility (Community Collection Center), Mon–Sat 7:30am–5pm. Source: hcfl.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Tampa, FL. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Hillsborough County Solid Waste Management
Full address
16180 West Lake Drive, Wimauma, FL 33598
Accepted materials
Electronics (computers, monitors, keyboards, routers, phones, TVs, printers, fax machines, radios, stereo components, VCR/DVD players); paint (latex and oil-based), stain and varnish; rechargeable batteries (individually bagged) and lead-acid batteries (auto, marine, motorcycle); tires; appliances and bulky items; scrap metal; recycling drop-off. Not accepted: alkaline batteries, commercial waste, large batteries over 12 x 12 in.
Hours / collection schedule
Mon–Sat 7:30am–5pm
Fees
Residential customers' annual solid waste assessment includes 10 cubic yards of bulky waste/appliance disposal per year and up to 12 tires per year; tires over 12 per year must go to the Southeast County Landfill at the tipping fee
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Hillsborough County Community Collection Center (CCC). Bring a photo ID with your Hillsborough County residential address (photo ID only for batteries, paint and electronics; no ID for recyclables). Tires, bulky waste and appliances are only for County Solid Waste customers and also need a printed or digital copy of your current property tax bill; residents of Tampa, Temple Terrace and Plant City who pay a city for waste service should use their city's options for tires and bulky items. Limits: 5 intact electronic items per household per month (monitors, keyboards, routers and mice not counted); 10 one-gallon and 5 five-gallon paint containers per household per month; 12 tires per year. Businesses are not authorized to use CCCs. Hand unloading only. Paint/electronics limits: https://hcfl.gov/residents/property-owners-and-renters/trash-and-recycling/discarding-paint-and-electronics. (813) 272-5680
