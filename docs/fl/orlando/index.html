Where can I drop off paint in Orlando?

Porter Transfer Station HHW at 1326 Good Homes Rd, Orlando, FL 32818 is listed as accepting paint. Paint/chemicals max 25 gal per vehicle. Orange County Landfill (HHW, electronics & tires) at 5901 Young Pine Road, Orlando, FL 32829 is listed as accepting paint. The listing records acceptance as: Household hazardous waste free for Orange County residents (paint products, pesticides, propane tanks up to 100 lb, cooking oil, fluorescent bulbs, car fluids, rechargeable batteries, household cleaners, pool chemicals) and electronics with a circuit board (phones, computers, tablets, TVs except rear-projection, VCRs, CD players, stereos); waste passenger tires (up to 8 per load); garbage, C&D debris, yard waste; freon appliances. Not accepted at HHW drop-off: tires, rear-projection TVs, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, flares, prescription drugs. Orange County Utilities Solid Waste. No commercial waste at the HHW drop-off; paint and chemicals limited to 25 gallons per vehicle. Vehicles/trailers under 30 ft use the small-vehicle drop-off (manual unloading only). HHW details: https://www.orangecountyfl.net/tabid/986/default.aspx. (407) 836-6601. Seminole County Central Transfer Station (HHW, electronics & tires) at 1950 State Road 419, Longwood, FL 32750 is listed as accepting paint. The listing records acceptance as: Household hazardous waste (paints, stains, sealants, thinners and strippers; pesticides and herbicides; pool and lawn chemicals; solvents; glues; mercury devices; fluorescent tubes and CFLs; disinfectants and corrosive liquids); used motor oil (up to 20 gal), antifreeze, car batteries, gasoline (up to 5 gal), brake/transmission/power-steering fluid; lead-acid, Ni-Cd, mercury, gel cell and lithium batteries; propane tanks; sharps in a sealed container; electronics (computers, monitors, TVs, printers, cell phones, audio-visual equipment); waste tires (limit 4 per person); cooking oil; household garbage, yard waste and recyclables. Seminole County Solid Waste Management Division. Businesses are not permitted to drop off hazardous materials through the HHW program. The HHW Tunnel and ReUse Shop (free usable household products, Mon–Sat 8am–4pm) are inside the transfer station. HHW item list: https://www.seminolecountyfl.gov/departments-services/environmental-services/solid-waste-management/hazardous-and-special-waste. 407-665-2262. Last checked 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-20. Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.
