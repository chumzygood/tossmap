Seminole County Landfill (tires & automotive fluids)

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Orlando, FL. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Seminole County Solid Waste Management Division
Full address
1930 E. Osceola Road, Geneva, FL 32732
Accepted materials
Waste tires (limit 10 per person; oversized tires charged by weight); used oil, oil filters, lead-acid batteries and propane tanks (the landfill takes automotive fluids and tires only, not other household hazardous waste); Class I and Class II waste; yard waste; construction and demolition debris; white goods and appliances; boats (after inspection, by appointment); asbestos (by appointment)
Hours / collection schedule
Daily 7:30am–5:30pm
Fees
Passenger tires $3.16 each (limit 10); oversized tires $211/ton covered; Class I, Class III and yard waste $41.63/ton covered, $83.25/ton uncovered (rates effective Sept 25, 2024). Cash and credit cards (card connectivity may occasionally be limited)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Seminole County Solid Waste Management Division; serves residents and commercial customers. For paint, chemicals and electronics, use the HHW Tunnel at the Central Transfer Station in Longwood. Rates: https://www.seminolecountyfl.gov/departments-services/environmental-services/solid-waste-management/rates
