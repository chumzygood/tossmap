McLeod Road Transfer Station

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Orlando, FL. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Orange County Utilities Solid Waste Division
Full address
5000 L.B. McLeod Road, Orlando, FL 32811
Accepted materials
Garbage (Class I solid waste); yard waste on Saturdays only. Tires not accepted at this time; C&D debris only at the Landfill
Hours / collection schedule
Mon–Sat 8am–5pm for non-account customers; yard waste Sat only 8am–5pm; closed Thanksgiving and Christmas
Fees
$56.40/ton garbage; yard waste charged at the Class I rate ($56.40/ton); $10 minimum charge (rates effective Oct 1, 2026)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Orange County Utilities transfer station. For household hazardous waste use the Orange County Landfill or Porter Transfer Station. (407) 836-6601
