Seminole County Central Transfer Station (HHW, electronics & tires)

Seminole County Central Transfer Station (HHW, electronics & tires) in Longwood, FL accepts paint, electronics, tires, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals. See hours below. Source: seminolecountyfl.gov.

Seminole County Central Transfer Station (HHW, electronics & tires) is at 1950 State Road 419, Longwood, FL 32750. Hours: Mon–Sat 7:30am–5:30pm; Household Hazardous Waste Tunnel Mon–Sat 8am–5pm. It accepts household hazardous waste (paints, stains, sealants, thinners and strippers; pesticides and herbicides; pool and lawn chemicals; solvents; glues; mercury devices; fluorescent tubes and CFLs; disinfectants and corrosive liquids); used motor oil (up to 20 gal), antifreeze, car batteries, gasoline (up to 5 gal), brake/transmission/power-steering fluid; lead-acid, Ni-Cd, mercury, gel cell and lithium batteries; propane tanks; sharps in a sealed container; electronics (computers, monitors, TVs, printers, cell phones, audio-visual equipment); waste tires (limit 4 per person); cooking oil; household garbage, yard waste and recyclables. Fees: HHW and electronics free for all Seminole County residents, including residents of incorporated cities. Passenger tires $3.16 each (limit 4); Class I waste and yard waste $41.63/ton covered, $83.25/ton uncovered (rates effective Sept 25, 2024). This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
