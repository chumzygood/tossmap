North Dade Trash and Recycling Center

Miami residents can drop off electronics, tires and motor oil at North Dade Trash and Recycling Center. See hours below. Source: miamidade.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Miami, FL. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Miami-Dade County Department of Solid Waste Management
Full address
21500 NW 47 Avenue, Miami, FL 33055
Accepted materials
Up to 4 standard automobile tires (no more than 48 in. diameter); used motor oil; used electronics (computer monitors and TVs, personal computers, keyboards, hard drives, printers, VCRs, audio/video equipment, cell phones, hand-held radios); household trash; tree and yard cuttings; up to 3 cubic yards of construction and demolition debris per day; white goods (stoves, refrigerators, water heaters, washers, dryers, freezers); mattresses; wooden and chain-link fence (up to 3 cubic yards). No garbage (food or kitchen waste)
Hours / collection schedule
Daily 7am–5:30pm; closed Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Independence Day and Christmas Day
Fees
No charge for Miami-Dade Department of Solid Waste Management residential waste fee customers
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Only residential waste fee customers in unincorporated Miami-Dade County and Aventura, Cutler Bay, Doral, Miami Gardens, Miami Lakes, Opa-locka, Palmetto Bay, Pinecrest, Sunny Isles Beach and Sweetwater may use the centers. Present a valid Florida driver's license or ID card (scanned to match an eligible property address; otherwise submit the TRC Access Form). No oversize vehicles: vans over 11,000 lb GVW, pickups over 15,000 lb GVW, or trailers larger than 10 ft x 6 ft x 6 ft. Questions: call 311
