Fairfax County I-66 Transfer Station (HHW & e-waste)

Fairfax County I-66 Transfer Station (HHW & e-waste) in Fairfax, VA accepts paint, electronics, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals. See hours below. Source: fairfaxcounty.gov.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Washington, DC. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Fairfax County Department of Public Works and Environmental Services
Full address
4618 West Ox Road, Fairfax, VA 22030
Accepted materials
Household hazardous waste: aerosols, antifreeze and automotive fluids, gasoline, motor oil, rechargeable batteries, cooking oil, pool chemicals, household cleaners, lawn and garden chemicals and pesticides, oil-based paint, paint thinners, stains and varnish, mercury devices, CFLs and fluorescent tubes, propane tanks up to 20 lb; e-waste (TVs, computers, gaming systems, keyboards, speakers, cords); separated recycling incl. used motor oil, antifreeze and automobile batteries; bicycles; textiles; household trash. Not accepted at HHW: ammunition, explosives, illegal fireworks, LED bulbs, radioactive materials, hazardous waste pharmaceuticals, propane tanks over 20 lb, compressed gas cylinders, business hazardous waste
Hours / collection schedule
Residents: trash, recycling and food scraps Mon–Fri 7am–5pm, Sat–Sun 7am–4pm. HHW and e-waste: Mon 7am–5pm; Tue and Thu 12–5pm; Fri 7am–5pm; Sat–Sun 7am–4pm; closed Wed. Holiday closures: see the county's holiday hours (complex closed Thanksgiving Day)
Fees
Household hazardous waste is free for Fairfax County residents; common 20 lb barbecue propane tanks are accepted for a fee
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Operated by Fairfax County Department of Public Works and Environmental Services (Fairfax County, Virginia; it serves Fairfax County residents, not DC residents). Free HHW disposal is for Fairfax County residents only; proof of residency may be required (driver's license, state ID, property tax bill or utility bill). Business hazardous waste is not accepted. Latex paint: let it dry and put it in the trash, or bring cans you can't dry to the HHW area. HHW program: https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/publicworks/recycling-trash/household-hazardous-waste; HHW questions 703-324-5230. I-66 Transfer Station 703-631-1179
