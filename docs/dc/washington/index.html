Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Washington, DC

No permanent HHW or e-cycling drop-off. Fort Totten takes up to 4 tires during regular hours. HHW and electronics are collected at registered DPW Special Waste Collection Events. Source: https://dpw.dc.gov/service/household-hazardous-waste-hhw-e-cycling-document-shredding

Fort Totten Transfer Station (tires)

4900 John McCormack Rd NE, Washington, DC 20011

Accepts: Passenger tires (up to 4); bulk trash, recycling, yard waste and appliances during regular hours
Hours: Tue–Fri 10am–2pm; Sat 7am–2pm; closed Mon & District holidays
Fees: Free for DC residents
Notes: DC residents only; proof of residency required. HHW and electronics are NOT accepted here — use Special Waste Collection Events.

DPW Special Waste Collection Event – RFK Stadium Lot 8

2500 Independence Ave SE, Washington, DC 20003

Accepts: HHW (paints, chemicals, batteries, CFLs, motor oil, auto fluids, propane ≤20 lb); e-waste (computers, TVs, phones, printers, monitors); document shredding (up to 5 boxes)
Hours: Event: Sat Nov 14, 2026, 8am–2pm (registration required; no entry after 2pm)
Fees: Free
Notes: DC residents only; register in advance on Eventbrite; bring proof of residency and registration. Next listed event after this: Sat Dec 12, 2026, also at RFK Lot 8.
