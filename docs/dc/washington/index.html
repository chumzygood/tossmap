Where can I drop off paint in Washington?

TossMap does not list a permanent drop-off for paint in Washington, DC. DPW Special Waste Collection Event – RFK Stadium Lot 8 at 2500 Independence Ave SE, Washington, DC 20003 is listed as accepting paint. The listing records acceptance as: HHW (paints, chemicals, batteries, CFLs, motor oil, auto fluids, propane ≤20 lb); e-waste (computers, TVs, phones, printers, monitors); document shredding (up to 5 boxes). Recorded hours: Event: Sat Nov 14, 2026, 8am–2pm (registration required; no entry after 2pm). This is a scheduled event, not a permanent drop-off site. DC residents only; register in advance on Eventbrite; bring proof of residency and registration. Next listed event after this: Sat Dec 12, 2026, also at RFK Lot 8. Last checked 2026-11-11. Household collection event or permanent drop-off?

In Washington, Fort Totten Transfer Station (tires) is listed as a standing drop-off site, and DPW Special Waste Collection Event – RFK Stadium Lot 8 is listed as a scheduled collection event. Do not treat an event date as year-round hours. Confirm both options on the official source before you go. Last checked 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11. Source: https://dpw.dc.gov/service/household-hazardous-waste-hhw-e-cycling-document-shredding.

Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.
