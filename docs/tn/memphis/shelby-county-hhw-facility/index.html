Shelby County HHW Facility

Shelby County HHW Facility in Memphis, TN accepts paint, electronics, batteries and household chemicals. See hours below. Source: shelbycountytn.gov.

Shelby County HHW Facility is at 6305 Haley Rd, Memphis, TN 38134. Hours: Tue, Thu, Sat 8am–1pm; 2026 holiday closings: Jan. 1, July 4, Thanksgiving Day (Nov. 26), and Christmas (listed on the official 2026 schedule as Thursday, Dec. 24). It accepts household hazardous waste from residential homes, including oil-based paint. Latex paint is not accepted (dry it and place it in regular trash). Computer screens, monitors and keyboards are accepted. Televisions and scrap metals are not accepted. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Eligibility, ID and other notes
Shelby County residents only (you will be asked for a car tag and zip code). An exception is made for members of the military, who are not required to present a Shelby County tag. Items should be brought in a clear tote or box. No commercial or business waste. Limit 100 pounds per vehicle every 30 days. Electronics limit: one monitor, one keyboard, and one computer per family every 30 days. Phone (901) 222-7729.
