Where can I drop off hazardous waste in Memphis?

TossMap lists 1 official drop-off site in Memphis, TN. Listed materials include paint, electronics, batteries and household chemicals. Shelby County residents only (you will be asked for a car tag and zip code). An exception is made for members of the military, who are not required to present a Shelby County tag. Items should be brought in a clear tote or box. No commercial or business waste. Limit 100 pounds per vehicle every 30 days. Electronics limit: one monitor, one keyboard, and one computer per family every 30 days. Phone (901) 222-7729. Last checked 2026-11-26. Confirm current hours, eligibility and acceptance with the official source before you go. Source: https://www.shelbycountytn.gov/439/Household-Hazardous-Waste.

Are the hours and fees guaranteed?

No. TossMap shows source links and last-checked dates so you can see where a listing came from and when it was reviewed. Hours, fees and accepted materials can change after that check. Confirm with the linked facility or government page before loading your vehicle and making the trip.

Can businesses use these drop-off sites?

Do not assume so. Many household hazardous-waste programs restrict drop-offs to residents and exclude commercial waste. Read the eligibility notes for the individual facility, then confirm with the linked government or operator source. A place listed near your business may still require a commercial disposal service or a separate appointment.
