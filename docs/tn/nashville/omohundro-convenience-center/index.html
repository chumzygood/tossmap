Omohundro Convenience Center

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Nashville, TN. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Metro Nashville Department of Waste Services
Full address
1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210
Accepted materials
Electronics (free, up to 100 lb per visit); cooking grease; mattresses and box springs ($12 each, 4 per month); construction and demolition material ($20, up to 2 cu yd); 3 bags of household trash plus 1 bulk item free; recycling and compostables. Household hazardous waste and tires go to East or Ezell Pike
Hours / collection schedule
Tue–Sat 8:30am–4:30pm (closed Metro holidays)
Fees
Electronics, recycling and 3 bags + 1 bulk item free; other loads $10 under 1 cu yd or $20 per cu yd; card fee 2.8% (min $1.95); cash not accepted here
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Davidson County residents only; show a Tennessee driver's license with a Davidson County address. No commercial vehicles or commercial waste. Hours/locations: https://www.nashville.gov/departments/waste-services/convenience-centers/hours-and-locations
