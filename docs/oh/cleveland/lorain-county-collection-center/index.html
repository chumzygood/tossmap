Lorain County Collection Center (HHW, tires & electronics)

Lorain County Collection Center (HHW, tires & electronics) in Elyria, OH accepts paint, electronics, tires, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals. See hours below. Source: loraincountyohio.gov.

Lorain County Collection Center (HHW, tires & electronics) is at 540 South Abbe Road, Elyria, OH 44035. Hours: Mon 9am–3pm; Wed noon–6pm; Sat 9am–3pm (subject to change). Holiday closures listed by the county include Sat Nov 28, Wed Dec 23, Sat Dec 26 and Wed Dec 30, 2026, and Sat Jan 2, 2027. It accepts household hazardous waste (residential only): acids and bases, adhesives, aerosol cans, vegetable oils, auto fluids (antifreeze, brake, hydraulic, power steering and transmission fluid, used oil and filters), batteries (button, lithium, rechargeable, lead-acid automotive), fuels, herbicides, pesticides and fertilizers, household cleaners, latex paint, paint thinner, MAPP gas and propane cylinders under 33 lb, mercury devices, pool chemicals, smoke detectors, solvents and sealers. Tires (residential only): automobile, bicycle or motorcycle tires up to 20 in. rim diameter. Electronics (residential and business): computers, TVs and monitors, cell phones, printers, hard drives, phones, radios, speakers, streaming devices, microwave ovens (the only appliance accepted) and more. Fluorescent bulbs and ballasts; general recyclables. Not accepted: appliances other than microwaves, air conditioners, dehumidifiers, ammunition and fireworks, asbestos, compressed gas cylinders, construction debris, prescription medications, sharps, tires over 20 in., trash. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
