South Euclid Service Garage (HHW drop-off)

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Cleveland, OH. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
City of South Euclid (Cuyahoga County Solid Waste District program)
Full address
4224 Monticello Boulevard, South Euclid, OH
Accepted materials
Household hazardous waste (oil-based paint, solvents, automotive fluids, aerosols, pesticides, pool chemicals, household cleaners). No latex paint
Hours / collection schedule
Year-round, Mon–Fri 8am–3pm
Fees
Free (Cuyahoga County HHW program)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Drop-off is at the city service garage; the District says most communities accept waste only from their own residents, so call the city before going. Cuyahoga County Solid Waste District HHW program (Ohio): free to all Cuyahoga County households; examples include oil- or solvent-based paint, solvents, automotive fluids, aerosols, pesticides, pool chemicals and household cleaners. The District's own facility is not open to the public.
