Medina County Recycling Center (specialty waste & HHW drop-off)

Medina County Recycling Center (specialty waste & HHW drop-off) in Seville, OH accepts paint, electronics, tires, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals. See hours below. Source: recyclemedinacounty.com.

Medina County Recycling Center (specialty waste & HHW drop-off) is at 8730 Lake Road, Seville, OH 44273. Hours: Specialty waste and HHW (including tires and electronics): Mon & Wed 8am–4:30pm; Sat 7am–noon. Trash and recycling: Mon–Fri 7:30am–4:30pm; Sat 7am–noon. Closed Sundays and New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas. It accepts household hazardous waste (aerosol cans, antifreeze, household cleaners, pesticides, oil-based paint, liquid latex paint, used motor oil); batteries (button-cell, coin, lithium, nickel-cadmium, nickel-metal hydride, lithium-ion, lead-acid); electronics (computer monitors, computer towers and hard drives, televisions, UPS battery backups, toner and ink cartridges); passenger, light-truck, oversized pickup and racing tires off the rim (no agricultural or semi tires); window air conditioners and other appliances with Freon. Not accepted: ammunition, asbestos. Fees: Tires $2.50 each (passenger and light truck), $13 each (oversized pickup and racing); computer monitors, computer towers and TVs $5 each; window air conditioners $10 each; aerosol cans, antifreeze, household cleaners and pesticides no charge. Cash or check only. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
