Westlake Service Center (HHW drop-off)

Westlake Service Center (HHW drop-off) in Westlake, OH accepts paint, electronics, tires and household chemicals; fees: Free (Cuyahoga County HHW program). See hours below. Source: cuyahogarecycles.org.

Westlake Service Center (HHW drop-off) is at 741 Bassett Road, Westlake, OH. Hours: Seasonal (2026): May 11–16 and Sep 14–19; Mon–Fri 8am–6pm, Sat 7:30am–noon. All 2026 windows have passed; the District has not posted 2027 dates yet. It accepts computer equipment including cell phones (no TVs); scrap tires (20 in. or less, no rims); household hazardous waste (oil-based paint, solvents, automotive fluids, aerosols, pesticides, pool chemicals, household cleaners); no latex paint. Fees: Free (Cuyahoga County HHW program). This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Cuyahoga County Solid Waste District HHW program (Ohio): free to all Cuyahoga County households; examples include oil- or solvent-based paint, solvents, automotive fluids, aerosols, pesticides, pool chemicals and household cleaners. The District's own facility is not open to the public.
