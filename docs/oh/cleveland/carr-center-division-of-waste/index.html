Carr Center (Division of Waste)

Cleveland residents can drop off electronics, motor oil and household chemicals at Carr Center (Division of Waste), and it's free. See hours below. Source: clevelandohio.gov.

Carr Center (Division of Waste) is at 5600 Carnegie Ave., Cleveland, OH 44103. Hours: Recycling, shredding & computer/cell-phone disposal: Mon–Fri 9am–3pm. HHW: first Friday of each month only, 9am–3pm (next: Fri Dec 4, 2026). It accepts E-waste: computers and cell phones (Mon–Fri; no TVs). HHW on first Fridays only: oil, cleaners, solvents, pesticides, household chemicals (latex paint not accepted). Fees: Free. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
