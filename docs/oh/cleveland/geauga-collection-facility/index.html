Geauga Collection Facility (HHW, electronics & appliances)

Geauga Collection Facility (HHW, electronics & appliances) in Chardon, OH accepts paint, electronics, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals. See hours below. Source: startrecycling.com.

Geauga Collection Facility (HHW, electronics & appliances) is at 12685 Merritt Road, Chardon, OH 44024. Hours: Fridays 10am–3pm, year-round; closed on government holidays. The 2026 once-a-month Saturday openings (May–October) have all passed. It accepts household hazardous waste: cleaning products, pesticides, flammables (gasoline, diesel, kerosene, lighter fluid), propane cylinders, lawn and garden chemicals, antifreeze, brake and transmission fluid, lead-acid car batteries, used and new motor oil and filters, oil-based paint, aerosol paint, paint thinner, stripper, varnish and other workshop supplies, single-use and rechargeable batteries, fluorescent bulbs and CFLs, mercury items, cooking oil, fire extinguishers. Electronics: TVs and monitors, computers, laptops and tablets, printers, cell phones, game consoles, cords, vacuums, power tools, small household appliances. Appliances, including refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners and other Freon appliances. Foam (Styrofoam). Not accepted: latex paint, tires, medical waste, joint compounds, acetylene or unknown tanks, drums, trailer loads, business waste. Fees: Free (the District says all items are accepted free of charge). This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
