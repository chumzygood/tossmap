Lake County Solid Waste Facility (e-waste, tires & Saturday HHW)

Lake County Solid Waste Facility (e-waste, tires & Saturday HHW) in Painesville, OH accepts paint, electronics, tires, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals. See hours below. Source: lakecountyohio.gov.

Lake County Solid Waste Facility (e-waste, tires & Saturday HHW) is at 2039 Blase Nemeth Road, Painesville, OH 44077. Hours: Mon–Fri 7am–3:30pm; Sat 9am–1pm (Saturdays for Lake County residents only). E-waste: all operating hours. Universal waste (household hazardous waste): Sat 9am–1pm only. It accepts E-waste: all residential electronics, including computers, laptops, tablets, hard drives, mice, keyboards, modems, routers, cables, printers, scanners, copiers, phones, TVs and monitors, and most other items that plug in or take batteries (not major appliances). Tires without rims (car and pickup up to 20 in., semi, tractor and agricultural; 6 max.). Saturdays, Lake County residents only: aerosol cans, antifreeze, motor oil and other automotive lubricants, lithium-ion, NiCad, lead-acid and alkaline batteries, fertilizer, fluorescent bulbs, mercury thermometers, thermostats and switches, oil-based paints and stains, solvents, paint thinners and strippers, pesticides, household cleaners and pool chemicals. Not accepted: latex paint (dry it and put it in the trash), propane and acetylene tanks, fire extinguishers, gasoline, kerosene, ammunition, medical waste. Fees: E-waste free except TVs and monitors ($5 each for 27 in. or smaller; $10 each for larger, projection and console TVs); tires $3 each (car and pickup), $5 each (semi), $15 each (tractor and agricultural); Saturday universal waste free; trash from $5 (car load) or $10 (pickup load). This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
