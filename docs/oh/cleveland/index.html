Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Cleveland, OH

Carr Center (Division of Waste)

5600 Carnegie Ave., Cleveland, OH 44103

Accepts: E-waste: computers and cell phones (Mon–Fri; no TVs). HHW on first Fridays only: oil, cleaners, solvents, pesticides, household chemicals (latex paint not accepted)
Hours: Recycling, shredding & computer/cell-phone disposal: Mon–Fri 9am–3pm. HHW: first Friday of each month only, 9am–3pm (next: Fri Dec 4, 2026)
Fees: Free
Notes: City of Cleveland residents only

Ridge Road Transfer Station

3727 Ridge Rd., Cleveland, OH 44144

Accepts: Passenger tires without rims (residents; up to 4 free dumps/year including tires; fee beyond 4 tires); bulky waste; recycling; HHW on first Fridays only (oil, cleaners, solvents, pesticides, household chemicals; latex paint not accepted)
Hours: Recycling: Mon–Sat 9am–3pm. Regular waste disposal: Sat 9am–3pm. HHW: first Friday of each month only, 9am–3pm (next: Fri Dec 4, 2026). Bulky/tire drop-off for residents (limits apply)
Fees: Free for qualifying resident drop-offs; fee may apply beyond tire/dump limits
Notes: City of Cleveland residents only; proof of residency required for drop-off
