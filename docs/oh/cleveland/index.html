Carr Center (Division of Waste)

5600 Carnegie Ave., Cleveland, OH 44103

Accepts: Household hazardous waste (oil, cleaners, solvents, pesticides, household chemicals) on first Fridays; computers and cell phones (e-waste) Mon–Fri
Hours: HHW: first Friday of each month only, 9am–3pm. Recycling, shredding & computer disposal: Mon–Fri 9am–3pm
Notes: City of Cleveland residents only; latex paint not accepted
