Cleveland Heights Service Center (computer drop-off)

Cleveland residents can drop off electronics at Cleveland Heights Service Center (computer drop-off), Computer equipment: year-round, Mon–Fri 7am–3:30pm. Source: cuyahogarecycles.org.

Cleveland Heights Service Center (computer drop-off) is at 14200 Superior Road, Cleveland Heights, OH. Hours: Computer equipment: year-round, Mon–Fri 7am–3:30pm. It accepts computer equipment, including cell phones. Scrap tires and household hazardous waste are not dropped off here: Cleveland Heights collects them curbside (call (216) 691-7300; HHW must be labeled, sealed and boxed; no latex paint; no tires on rims). This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
