Orange Village Service Department (HHW drop-off)

Orange Village Service Department (HHW drop-off) in Orange Village, OH accepts paint and household chemicals, First Friday of every month, 7am–3pm; fees: Free (Cuyahoga County HHW program). Source: cuyahogarecycles.org.

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Cleveland, OH. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Orange Village (Cuyahoga County Solid Waste District program)
Full address
4680 Lander Road, Orange Village, OH
Accepted materials
Household hazardous waste (oil-based paint, solvents, automotive fluids, aerosols, pesticides, pool chemicals, household cleaners). No latex paint
Hours / collection schedule
First Friday of every month, 7am–3pm
Fees
Free (Cuyahoga County HHW program)
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Drop-off is at the village service department; the District says most communities accept waste only from their own residents, so call the city before going. Service Department (440) 498-4403. Cuyahoga County Solid Waste District HHW program (Ohio): free to all Cuyahoga County households; examples include oil- or solvent-based paint, solvents, automotive fluids, aerosols, pesticides, pool chemicals and household cleaners. The District's own facility is not open to the public.
