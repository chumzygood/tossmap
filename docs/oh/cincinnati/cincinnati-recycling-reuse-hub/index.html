Cincinnati Recycling & Reuse Hub

Waste and recycling drop-off information for Cincinnati, OH. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.

Before you go

Operator
Cincinnati Recycling & Reuse Hub
Full address
911 Evans Street, Cincinnati, OH 45204
Accepted materials
E-waste (computers, laptops, phones, printers, speakers, home entertainment, mixed electronics); TVs and monitors; car and bike tires (on or off rim) and bike inner tubes; batteries; lightbulbs (LED, CFL, tube)
Hours / collection schedule
Tue 10am–2pm; Thu 12–6pm; Sat 10am–2pm (closed Nov 26–28 and Dec 22–Jan 1)
Fees
Electronics: small amounts free, car trunk $10 / SUV $15; TVs $5–$40; car tire off-rim $10 / on-rim $25; bike tire $5–$10
Eligibility, ID and other notes
Park on Woodrow Street (rear of building). Listed in Hamilton County Resource outlets directory. Household chemical HHW is NOT accepted here — county HHW is event-based.
