Where to dump hazardous waste, paint & e-waste in Cincinnati, OH

Hamilton County's HHW chemical program is event-based with registration; no permanent public HHW site. Year-round tires and e-waste: Cincinnati Recycling & Reuse Hub. Source: https://hamiltoncountyresource.org/recycling_guide/hazardous_products.php

Cincinnati Recycling & Reuse Hub

911 Evans Street, Cincinnati, OH 45204

Accepts: E-waste (computers, laptops, phones, printers, speakers, home entertainment, mixed electronics); TVs and monitors; car and bike tires (on or off rim) and bike inner tubes; batteries; lightbulbs (LED, CFL, tube)
Hours: Tue 10am–2pm; Thu 12–6pm; Sat 10am–2pm (closed Nov 26–28 and Dec 22–Jan 1)
Fees: Electronics: small amounts free, car trunk $10 / SUV $15; TVs $5–$40; car tire off-rim $10 / on-rim $25; bike tire $5–$10
Notes: Park on Woodrow Street (rear of building). Listed in Hamilton County Resource outlets directory. Household chemical HHW is NOT accepted here — county HHW is event-based.
