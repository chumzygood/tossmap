SWACO Household Hazardous Waste Drop-Off

SWACO Household Hazardous Waste Drop-Off in Columbus, OH accepts paint, batteries, motor oil and household chemicals, Mon–Fri 9am–5pm; 1st Sat 9am–2pm. Source: swaco.org.
