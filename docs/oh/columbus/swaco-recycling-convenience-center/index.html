SWACO Recycling Convenience Center

SWACO Recycling Convenience Center in Columbus, OH accepts paint, electronics, tires, batteries and motor oil. See hours below. Source: swaco.org.

SWACO Recycling Convenience Center is at 2566 Jackson Pike, Columbus, OH 43223. Hours: Mon–Fri 9:30am–5pm; Sat 9am–4pm (see SWACO's 2026 holiday schedule for limited hours and closures). It accepts tires (fee); car batteries; motor oil, oil filters and antifreeze; electronics: CRT and flat computer monitors, CRT and LCD TVs (fees), household electronics including cell phones, printers and VCRs, string lights; household batteries including alkaline and lithium-ion, and power batteries; paint: latex, oil-based and spray paint; fluorescent bulbs and CFLs; appliances (including Freon appliances); fats, oils and grease; food scraps; select yard waste; scrap metal; bicycles; Styrofoam; paper shredding; mattresses and box springs (until Nov 30, 2026); basic recycling (cardboard, paper, cartons, glass, cans, plastic bottles). Not accepted: soil or dirt, treated wood. Fees: Fees for some items: tires $5.00 each; LCD TV $15.00; CRTs under 20 in. $25.00, under 35 in. $35.00, over 35 in. $45.00 (fees subject to change). Credit, debit or check only; 3% card processing fee. This is an independent source-backed listing, not the operator’s website.
